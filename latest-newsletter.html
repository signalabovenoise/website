Sector Highlights

IT +2.69%

The IT sector showed a modest gain of 1.26% in the week ending August 15, 2025, with key companies like Infosys and Mphasis rising up to 3% amid positive sentiment ahead of the US Federal Reserve's Jackson Hole conference. However, the broader IT index has had laggards this year, reflecting mixed performance overall despite short-term gains, possibly due to global economic uncertainties impacting demand for IT services.
FMCG +1.39%

The FMCG sector did not feature prominently among the top weekly gainers as of mid-August 2025, suggesting a relatively muted performance. The sector’s performance may be affected by inflation trends in India (1.55% in July 2025) and changing consumer spending patterns, though specific recent news for FMCG was limited in the latest data.
Realty +1.06%

Realty sector trends were not highlighted among major gainers or losers in the recent week ending August 15, 2025. Given the absence of significant sectoral movement in the summaries, Realty appears to be stable or experiencing lackluster demand, likely influenced by macroeconomic factors such as interest rates (steady at 5.50%) and moderate inflation.
Pharma -0.44%

The Pharma sector outperformed notably with a 3.60% gain in the week to August 15, 2025. However, individual stocks like Aurobindo Pharma saw declines amid specific stock news. Pharma’s strong sectoral gain may be driven by ongoing healthcare demand and possible regulatory or innovation developments enhancing positive investor sentiment.
Media -1.98%

The Media sector did not see specific mentions of significant gains or losses in the latest weekly sectoral data around mid-August 2025. This could indicate a neutral or slightly subdued performance day-to-day, possibly due to regulatory concerns, changing advertising spends, or shifts in digital content consumption patterns as reflected in cautious market trading.
