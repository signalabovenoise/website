Top News Developments
Landmark India-US Trade Deal Triggers Record Market Rally
  • Reciprocal tariffs have been slashed from 50% to 18%, removing a multi-year overhang for Indian exporters and technology firms.
  • India's commitment to purchase over $500 billion in US energy, tech, and agriculture products reshapes the geopolitical economic landscape.
  • GIFT Nifty’s 600-point overnight surge confirms a shift in market psychology from post-budget caution to aggressive growth optimism.
  • Sectoral winners include IT services, textiles, and auto ancillaries, which are now poised for significant volume growth and margin expansion.
Dalal Street Shrugs Off Budget STT Hikes for Capex Growth
  • The market has successfully pivoted from the disappointment of increased F&O taxes to the long-term benefits of a ₹12.2 lakh crore capex outlay.
  • Banking and metal sectors have provided the necessary muscle to keep the Nifty above the critical 25,500 psychological support mark.
  • Strategic purchases in oil and gas companies like Indian Oil Corporation are being driven by lower global crude prices and trade deal tailwinds.
  • Analysts view the recent volatility as a 'cleaning of the slate,' allowing for a more sustainable rally based on fundamental capital formation.
Export Heavies and Tech Majors Lead Sectoral Rotation
  • IT service leaders like Infosys and Persistent have seen a sharp rally as the US trade deal clarifies export prospects to North America.
  • The 'China Plus One' strategy is gaining renewed momentum as lower US tariffs make Indian specialty chemicals and generic drugs more competitive.
  • Electronic Manufacturing Services (EMS) firms like Avalon and Syrma are seeing improved earnings visibility due to policy stability in their primary US markets.
  • Textile exporters such as Gokaldas Exports are expected to see immediate margin boosts as they regain competitive parity with global peers.

Policy & Regulations

  • A historic India-US trade deal has slashed reciprocal tariffs from 50% to 18%, a major policy shift expected to add over ₹20 lakh crore to investor wealth by removing export barriers.
  • SEBI has significantly optimized the onboarding process for Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPIs), reducing the registration timeline from 30 days to just 5 days to attract global liquidity.
  • New regulatory guidelines now treat share buybacks as capital gains, a move seen as favorable for retail investors as it potentially simplifies tax calculations and post-tax return expectations.
  • Despite the broad trade easing, India maintains a protective stance on sensitive agricultural and dairy sectors, limiting the impact of global trade liberalization on domestic agri-players.
