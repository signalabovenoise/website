Fundamentals Corner
While the company boasts stable sales growth and excellent, accelerating Return on Equity (38.5% Last Year), its profit growth has been highly erratic, marked by a significant -38% YoY net profit decline in the latest quarter despite robust operating profit growth.
This sharp drop in recent net profits, juxtaposed with strong historical stock price performance and an attractive 7.24% dividend yield, suggests a critical disconnect between operational strength and bottom-line profitability.
Retail investors should thoroughly investigate the specific drivers of this recent net profit deterioration to assess the sustainability of its strong ROE and overall valuation amidst such earnings volatility.

Top News Developments
FIIs Pull Record Rs 1.58 Lakh Crore from Indian Equities in 2025
  • Foreign institutional investors (FIIs) recorded their largest-ever net equity outflows from India in 2025, totaling over Rs 1.58 lakh crore, a move primarily driven by rupee depreciation and global capital shifts towards higher-yield destinations.
  • This massive selling significantly pressured domestic valuations, particularly large-cap stocks, and reduced overall market liquidity, impacting key rupee-sensitive sectors like IT, pharma, and banks.
  • Despite these outflows, India's capital markets demonstrated resilience, with robust retail participation and rising SIP flows offsetting some of the FII selling, signaling a structural shift towards more disciplined domestic investing.
  • While the short-term outlook remains bearish due to continued profit-booking and liquidity squeeze, this year-end repositioning could set the stage for a potential rebound in 2026, contingent on global interest rate easing and renewed investor confidence.
  • Retail investors should recognize that such FII exits can create attractive buying opportunities in fundamentally strong large-cap stocks that may have been unfairly penalized, but they must also be prepared for continued volatility.
Year-End Profit-Taking Drags Indian Markets Lower in Shortened Week
  • Indian markets concluded a holiday-shortened week on a lower note, with Sensex and Nifty seeing declines, primarily due to widespread year-end profit-taking as investors locked in gains near all-time highs amid an absence of fresh catalysts.
  • This consolidation tempered optimism for a 'Santa Claus rally' and reinforced a period of range-bound trading, particularly affecting high-beta sectors like financials, metals, and consumer goods, which saw broad profit-booking.
  • The market's performance highlights a cautious sentiment heading into the new year, despite underlying positive undertones from domestic demand and liquidity, and it underscores the domestic investor resilience even as foreign flows exited.
  • A notable fall in India VIX to record lows signals potential market complacency, cautioning retail investors against overconfidence and emphasizing the importance of prudent risk management.
  • While bearish in the immediate days due to continued consolidation, the market could see a mixed outlook over weeks/quarters, with fresh momentum expected from 2026 consumer tech IPOs and potential policy easing.
Zepto IPO Fuels Optimism for Consumer Tech Sector in 2026
  • The highly anticipated early-2026 IPO of quick commerce firm Zepto, aiming for a $1.3 billion listing, is poised to redirect significant investor focus and capital towards the consumer tech sector in India.
  • This development highlights the unique scale and potential of India's quick commerce market, which remains largely unparalleled globally, signaling sustained investment interest despite inherent margin pressures and intense competition among players.
  • For the broader market, this influx of liquidity into consumer tech listings could reinforce themes of premiumisation and rising income growth, partially offsetting some of the impact from recent FII outflows by attracting domestic and primary market investments.
  • The success of Zepto's IPO could catalyze further listings in the new-age tech space, creating new opportunities for retail investors to participate in high-growth segments of the digital economy.
  • While bullish for sector leaders like Zomato due to increased demand visibility, retail investors should carefully evaluate individual company fundamentals, competitive landscape, and the path to profitability within this aggressive, growth-focused sector.

Policy & Regulations

  • SEBI has mandated a uniform brokerage fee structure for all stock exchange members, eliminating member-specific discounts that previously encouraged fee arbitrage, which is expected to intensify cost-competition among brokers and potentially reduce trading costs for retail investors.
  • The regulator imposed a temporary trading ban on Jane Street Group, a significant liquidity provider, which immediately caused a nearly 10% drop in the National Stock Exchange's equity-derivatives index and cut total options premium turnover by roughly 40% on the first post-ban expiry day, highlighting the critical role of market makers in liquidity and price stability.
  • SEBI continues to enforce its short-selling restrictions for foreign portfolio investors, limiting short positions to stocks with less than 2% foreign ownership and those not on the RBI's caution list; this rule aims to curtail speculative pressure on vulnerable equities and protect domestic market stability.
  • The government is considering a manufacturing-focused incentive scheme under the Drone Shakti initiative in Union Budget 2026-27 to boost indigenous drone production, which could create significant opportunities for domestic manufacturers and technology firms in the aerospace and defense sectors. Additionally, new Colliery Control rules have been notified to simplify and accelerate coal mining operations by allowing company boards to approve mine openings, streamlining processes for the energy and industrial sectors.
