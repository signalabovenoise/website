Market Summary

Daily Overview

  • On October 8, 2025, the Indian stock market saw a mild correction with the Sensex falling 153 points (0.19%) to close at 81,773.66, and the Nifty slipping below 25,100 amid profit booking after a four-day winning streak.
  • The overall market sentiment among retail investors appeared cautious due to the profit booking and mild weakness in banking and financial shares, despite some resilience in IT stocks ahead of earnings announcements.
  • The market's short-term pullback reflected broader concerns over valuation levels after recent gains, prompting investors to consolidate positions and await further macroeconomic signals.
  • No single dominant sector drove the market movement, as the decline was mainly led by financials with realty and media stocks also witnessing selling pressure.
  • Market participants remained attentive to upcoming quarterly results and domestic policy developments that may influence near-term trends, reflecting a wait-and-watch approach post recent strong rallies.

  • The indices dipped as profit-booking took hold post-rally, with financials, banks, autos, and metals driving losses.
  • Global risk-off sentiment led to positioning into IT and defensives ahead of Q2 earnings season.

Rupee Weakens to Near Record Low Against the US Dollar
  • The rupee closed near its all-time low of ₹88.80 against USD, pressured by global dollar strength and crude oil prices.
  • Export-oriented sectors (IT, pharma) gained short-term benefits while import-heavy industries faced input cost pressures.
  • Sustained rupee weakness could force RBI intervention, impacting liquidity and investor sentiment.
Titan Delivers Strong Q2 Business Update, Stock Jumps Over 4%
