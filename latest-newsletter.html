Top News Developments
The AI Scare Trade: IT Sector’s 20% February Meltdown
  • Nifty IT plummeted over 20% this month as fears grew that Anthropic’s Claude AI could disrupt legacy software maintenance and enterprise services.
  • A contagion sell-off triggered by IBM’s largest single-day drop in 25 years severely impacted heavyweights like TCS, Infosys, and HCLTech.
  • While the current rout is narrative-driven, cybersecurity stocks also fell as AI tools demonstrated capabilities to automatically fix open-source vulnerabilities.
  • Some analysts argue IT firms will eventually act as bridges for AI adoption, but near-term valuation resets are unavoidable as clients reassess spending.
Trade War 2.0: US Tariff Volatility Hits Indian Exports
  • Proposed 10-15% universal US tariffs under a potential Trump administration have heightened defensive sentiment across export-reliant Indian sectors.
  • Textile players like Gokaldas Exports and Trident face a double whammy of reduced duty benefits and rising uncertainty regarding US market access.
  • A legal vacuum in the US may lead to the 'front-loading' of exports in machinery and textiles as firms try to beat potential future tariff spikes.
  • Capital is noticeably shifting toward safe havens like gold and US Treasuries as investors hedge against potential global trade disruptions.
Infrastructure Momentum: Record Orders and Global JVs
  • Power sector firms like KEC International are reporting record order intakes of ₹21,300 crore YTD, driven by national grid strengthening.
  • Cochin Shipyard's $500-million joint venture with HD Hyundai marks the first direct investment by a global shipbuilding giant in Indian infrastructure.
  • The 'LIGO India' project involving L&T highlights India's growing role in global mega-science, creating high-tech manufacturing opportunities.
  • Adani Power faces regional risks as Bangladesh’s new leadership seeks to renegotiate power purchase agreements, impacting cross-border infrastructure sentiment.
Earnings Duality: Banking Resilience vs. Retail Sector Hikes
  • HDFC Bank and SBI led a cluster of banking gainers today, riding the surge in credit growth and signals of a modest RBI rate cut.
  • R R Kabel stood out with a 42.3% revenue jump, underscoring robust demand for infrastructure-grade cables despite broader market weakness.
  • India Inc. is projected to offer 9.1% salary hikes in 2026, though firms are increasingly balancing labor costs against AI-driven automation needs.
  • The impending removal of certain stocks from the F&O segment by May 2026 is expected to lead to structural selling and reduced liquidity in specific counters.
