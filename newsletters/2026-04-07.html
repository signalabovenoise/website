Top News Developments
RBI Intervenes as Rupee Volatility Hits Multi-Year Highs
  • Tighter NDF rules and bank oversight aim to stabilize the rupee following a 10% slide against the USD in FY26.
  • The intervention prioritizes forex stability over aggressive reserve depletion to maintain market confidence.
  • Tighter liquidity rules may temporarily raise short-term bond yields and increase hedging costs for importers.
IT Sector Becomes Tactical Hedge Against Geopolitical Turmoil
  • Value buying in beaten-down IT majors like TCS and Infosys provided the primary momentum for the 1,122-point Sensex rebound.
  • Weaker rupee dynamics are providing a 2-4% EPS tailwind for IT exporters, offsetting global discretionary spending concerns.
  • Samsung's massive profit jump serves as a positive global proxy for Indian IT's potential growth in AI-related service segments.
FII Selling Reaches Systemic Proportions in Financial Services
  • FPIs divested over ₹1.17 lakh crore in March, with nearly 50% of the outflows concentrated in the banking and financial services sector.
  • Despite the exodus, large-cap banks like HDFC and ICICI are seeing upgrades from brokerages citing 'attractive contrarian' entry points.
  • Heavyweight portfolio shifts, such as Motilal Oswal increasing weight in SBI over HDFC Bank, highlight a focus on growth-to-valuation dynamics.
Consumption and Retail Face Headwinds from Cost Inflation and Over-Expansion
  • FMCG companies like Godrej Consumer are bracing for up to 9% cost inflation in palm oil, necessitating calibrated price hikes.
  • Trent’s 50% stock correction reflects growing investor anxiety over the 'over-densification' and potential cannibalization of its Zudio store network.
  • In contrast, the organized jewelry and microfinance sectors show resilience, with Kalyan Jewellers and CreditAccess Grameen reporting strong growth decoupled from macro gloom.
