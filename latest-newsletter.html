Sector Highlights

PSU Bank +1.70%

PSU banks rose as the broader banking segment held up better than the market, with sector rotation still favoring banks and PSU banks retaining relative strength after a long period of outperformance. Recent RRG commentary also notes PSU bank leadership has been strong, though it is beginning to slow, which fits a day of gains in a still-favored pocket of the market.

Bank +0.88%

Banks were higher, even as the broader market saw pressure, suggesting the move was driven by continued relative resilience in financials rather than a sector-specific negative shock. NSE index data shows NIFTY Bank was down in absolute terms on the day, but the sector’s relative positioning remains stronger than many cyclical groups, consistent with ongoing rotation into financials.

CPSE +0.38%

CPSE shares edged up, likely reflecting selective buying in state-owned and energy-linked names rather than a broad, sharp catalyst. The available market commentary points to sector rotation within defensive and public-sector themes, with CPSE not among the day’s weak groups, which supports a modest positive move rather than a headline-driven rally.

Realty -1.39%

Realty fell as the sector remained under support-zone pressure in relative-strength analysis, indicating weak demand and continued rotation away from property names. NSE data also shows NIFTY Realty lower on the day, consistent with a market that was not rewarding rate-sensitive or domestic cyclicals despite the broader sector rotation backdrop.

IT -5.57%

IT dropped sharply, driven by sector weakness rather than company-specific news in the available results. NSE data shows NIFTY IT under pressure on the day, while the market commentary does not indicate a fresh positive catalyst for exporters or software names, suggesting risk-off selling and rotation away from the sector.

Fundamentals Corner
The company’s core fundamentals are weakening as a 47% YoY collapse in operating profit reveals that recent net profit spikes are likely driven by non-operational income rather than business strength. This uneven momentum—where quarterly expenses grew 91% compared to just 20% in sales—explains why the stock price is down 12% despite the high TTM profit growth figure.
With a consistently stagnant 9% ROE and poor capital efficiency (5.73% ROCE), the current 20x P/E valuation appears detached from operational reality and lacks the fundamental support needed for a sustainable price recovery.
News TidBits
  • The government’s announcement of an Offer for Sale (OFS) in NHPC at an 8% discount triggered immediate price pressure, leading to a significant intraday slide.
  • The share sale created a short-term liquidity overhang that spilled over into other PSU power names like NTPC and Power Grid as investors feared further disinvestment supply.
  • Institutional demand for the OFS remains a key indicator of market appetite for high-yielding PSU defensives during periods of large-cap IT rotation.
  • Near-term performance depends on the stock clearing the OFS supply, though long-term growth is supported by NHPC’s strategic role in India's renewable energy expansion.
  • Investors should view this supply-led volatility as a tactical event rather than a fundamental deterioration of the company’s hydroelectric power dominance.

Top News Developments
IT Heavyweights Lead Index Recovery Amid AI Optimism
  • TCS, Infosys, and HCL Tech surged between 4% and 6.7%, acting as the primary support for the Sensex after a volatile trading stretch.
  • The rally was driven by a 'buy-the-dip' rotation into dollar-earning sectors as markets re-evaluate the multi-year AI software implementation cycle.
  • Investors are shifting focus from traditional volume-based IT models toward high-margin consumption-based AI billing opportunities.
  • The outperformance of IT services provides a defensive cushion against the weakness currently seen in domestic-focused PSU and utility stocks.
Auto Sector Duality: EV Momentum vs. Earnings Warnings
  • Tata Motors shares slumped 5.2% following a profit warning for Q2, citing weaker domestic passenger vehicle sales and rising raw material costs.
  • In contrast, the broader auto sector shows strong 24% YoY revenue growth, supported by the government's fleet replacement policy for EVs and BS6 vehicles.
  • GST rationalization is accelerating a structural shift from unorganized spare parts to organized ancillary brands, benefiting formal sector players.
  • Retail investors should differentiate between OEMs with immediate margin pressures and those leading the multi-year EV transition.
Oil Volatility Impacts OMCs and Macro Stability
  • Easing geopolitical concerns between the US and Iran helped pull oil off recent highs, offering a short-term reprieve for Indian markets.
  • Lower crude prices directly benefit Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs), paints, and chemicals sectors by easing raw material cost pressures.
  • Persistent volatility in oil futures continues to complicate the RBI’s policy outlook as energy-led inflation remains a primary concern.
  • India is aggressively diversifying its crude sources from Russia and Venezuela to mitigate Middle East supply risks as the era of cheap crude ends.
PSU Power Sentiment Hit by Supply Overhang
  • The NHPC stake sale triggered broader selling in the PSU basket, with NTPC and Power Grid also witnessing significant declines.
  • Investors are cautious that the NHPC OFS might signal a wider disinvestment pipeline, potentially creating more supply-led price caps in the near term.
  • Despite the pressure, robust domestic cargo volumes at Adani Ports suggest that underlying industrial trade activity remains strong.
  • The shift in flows from PSUs to IT heavyweights highlights a tactical rotation toward sectors with higher earnings visibility.

Emerging Opportunities

Indian IT services after the AI pullback, because clients still fund transformation budgets, offering a contrarian entry in large-cap exporters while sentiment remains crowded against them.
Mid-cap defense electronics, because order conversion is now visible, and the market still values only headline platforms, creating room for rerating in components and subsystems.
Textiles and apparel exporters, because tariff relief can lift realizations, and current demand skepticism leaves early-cycle valuations below replacement costs.
Domestic credit bureaus and collections tech, because fintech lending volumes keep rising, and underwriting data becomes the bottleneck as unsecured loan stress builds.
Capital goods for power transmission, because grid capex and renewable integration stay active, and the market underprices multi-year execution visibility in cable and transformer names.

Policy & Regulations

  • The newly effective India-Oman CEPA trade pact is expected to provide preferential tariff treatment, boosting bilateral trade and supply-chain resilience for Indian exporters.
  • The government is reportedly considering cutting taxes and removing ownership caps on specific bonds to attract more global capital and stimulate market liquidity.
  • New import duties on solar glass from Malaysia are creating a competitive tailwind for domestic manufacturers like Borosil Renewables, supporting the local green energy ecosystem.
  • Stricter GST 2.0 regimes, particularly steep cigarette tax hikes, are creating significant headwinds for ITC, leading the stock to hit 52-week lows as volume growth projections are re-evaluated.
  • The government’s coal-import policy, which caps imports at 10 million tonnes, is impacting domestic giants like Coal India by shifting demand dynamics for domestically produced coal.
