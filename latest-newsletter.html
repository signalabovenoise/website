Market Summary

Daily Overview

  • The BSE Sensex closed higher by 136 points (0.17%) at 81,926, with the NSE Nifty50 rising 30.65 points (0.12%) to 25,108.3 on 7 October 2025, extending the market's winning streak amid overall positive sentiment.
  • Broader markets also saw gains with the Nifty Midcap 100 index up 0.47% and the Nifty Smallcap 100 index up 0.31%, indicating strength beyond the leading large caps.
  • Sectorally, the Nifty Realty index was the top performer, gaining 1.09%, followed by Oil & Gas, Pharma, and Consumer Durables, while FMCG and PSU Banks faced selling pressure, showing mixed sectoral trends.
  • Retail investor sentiment remained cautiously optimistic supported by steady buying interest, although divergences between sectors suggested selective buying rather than broad-based enthusiasm.
  • Domestic institutional investors helped sustain market strength despite some foreign investor outflows, providing stability to the market environment.

Top News Developments
Finance Minister Addresses Global Fintech Fest 2025
  • Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman emphasized India's ambitious fintech roadmaps and regulatory support at the Global Fintech Fest 2025.
  • Potential discussions around easing of compliance norms and innovative policies could benefit digital payment companies and financial inclusion startups.
  • A positive sentiment in fintech stocks like Paytm and Infosys is likely, following optimism for liquidity boosts and technological advancements.
GST Cuts Propel FMCG Revenue Growth
  • Recent GST reforms lowering tariffs on essential FMCG products have created favorable conditions for revenue acceleration in players like Dabur and Tata Consumer.
  • Improved affordability and demand are boosting participation across rural consumption sectors, strengthening stock positions in allied industries.
  • This move aligns with government mandates to stimulate grassroots economic growth and stabilize inflation-sensitive outlooks.
Institutional Investments Highlight India’s Real-Estate Growth
  • Q3 data shows institutional inflows into Indian real estate rising 11%, driven by large office asset acquisitions.
  • Weakness in residential leasing is offset by continued demand for commercial properties, as urbanization drives workspace expansions.
  • This trend indicates durable confidence in real estate, favoring stocks linked to commercial REITs and construction majors like Brigade Enterprises.
Gold’s Target Revised Amid Geopolitical Uncertainty
  • Goldman Sachs has increased gold price targets to $4,900 by December 2026, citing ETF demand and sustained central bank purchases.
  • Geopolitical uncertainties are further enhancing gold's appeal as a hedge, making positions in Indian MCX-linked stocks and ETFs attractive.
  • Retail investors may find upside in gold-related assets amid dollar fluctuations and inflationary concerns.
Hexaware Technologies Declares Dividend Amid Strong Buy Rating
  • Hexaware Technologies announced a ₹5.75 dividend, underscoring strong Q2 growth performance and consistent revenue streams.

  • Dividend announcements typically create positive sentiment, attracting both income-focused and growth investors.
