Top News Developments
Union Budget 2026 STT Hike Triggers ₹11 Lakh Crore Market Rout
  • The Nifty fell approximately 2% on Budget Day, plunging to its September swing low of 24,587 following the substantial increase in STT on futures and options.
  • India VIX jumped 12.9% to 15.10, while the Put-Call Ratio slipped to 0.59, signaling heightened institutional fear and defensive positioning.
  • Exchanges and brokers like BSE and Angel One face direct earnings risk from expected declines in high-frequency trading and retail derivative volumes.
  • The move aims to curb speculative activity but risks compressing market depth and increasing execution costs for all participants.
FII Hedging Reversal & Persistent Currency Pressure
  • Returning from the Sunday holiday, FIIs aggressively added put longs and call shorts, indicating a lack of conviction in current market valuations.
  • The Indian Rupee is approaching the 92 mark, forcing aggressive RBI intervention to defend against global risk-off sentiment and capital outflows.
  • FIIs net sold over ₹41,000 crore in January, driven by India's valuation premium and the absence of fresh 'big-bang' reforms in the recent Budget.
  • The 'vicious cycle' of FII outflows and currency weakness remains a primary headwind for large-cap sectors like IT and Banking.
Bank Nifty Technical Breakdown Amid Record Government Borrowing
  • Bank Nifty closed below the critical 58,500 mark, with the 58,000 level (100-day moving average) now acting as a vital 'line in the sand' for technical traders.
  • The government's ₹17.2 lakh crore gross borrowing plan has pushed 10-year yields to 6.78%, threatening bank treasury books and net interest margins.
  • A divergence is emerging where PSU banks like SBI show better resilience than private peers due to their exposure to government infrastructure spending.
  • Continued pressure on the 58,000 level could drag the banking index toward 57,000, signaling a broader credit growth slowdown.

Policy & Regulations

  • The Union Budget 2026 implemented a sharp hike in Securities Transaction Tax (STT) on futures and options trades to 0.05%, a move intended to curb retail speculation that has immediately pressured market liquidity.
  • New proposals aim to eliminate tax deductions for interest on borrowed funds used to invest in dividend-paying stocks, likely discouraging leveraged investment strategies used by High Net-worth Individuals.
  • The Finance Bill 2026 has classified stockbroking services for Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPIs) as 'zero-rated exports,' effectively reducing the GST burden for Indian brokers serving overseas institutional clients.
  • A transformative 100% tax holiday until 2047 has been granted to data center infrastructure, positioning India as a global AI hub and providing a long-term fiscal tailwind for specialized tech and electrical firms.
  • The cigarette industry, led by ITC and Godfrey Phillips, faces immediate volume and margin pressure following a steep excise duty hike that has increased retail prices by approximately ₹22-25 per pack.
