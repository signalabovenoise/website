Market Summary

Daily Overview

  • The Indian stock market on 29 September 2025 ended largely flat with the Sensex and Nifty showing minor fluctuations, indicating a cautious trading sentiment among investors.
  • The NSE Nifty50 closed at approximately 24,635, down around 0.08%, while the Sensex moved slightly higher or traded mixed during the day, reflecting indecision in the market.
  • Retail investor sentiment appeared reserved, with the broader market indices such as MidCap and SmallCap showing modest gains, suggesting selective buying interest in mid and small-cap stocks.
  • Market commentary noted gains in oil & gas and public sector banks but avoided broad sector-specific bullishness, reflecting an overall muted mood without strong directional conviction.
  • Key economic news impacting investor sentiment included government assurances about meeting fiscal deficit and growth targets for FY26, adding a layer of fundamental stability to market outlook.

Sector Highlights

PSU Bank +1.78%

PSU Bank sector rose 1.78% driven mainly by positive buying interest in State Bank of India and other public sector banks amid expectations of improved asset quality and stable credit growth outlook in Q2 results announcements today.

PSE +1.17%

The Public Sector Enterprises (PSE) index gained 1.17% supported by government announcements reinforcing policy support and operational efficiencies across PSU companies, along with a steady performance outlook for state-run enterprises.

Realty sector advanced 0.88% due to an increase in demand indicators from recent urban infrastructure developments and easing of regulatory approvals facilitating faster project launches and sales.

Auto -0.18%

Media -0.85%

Fundamental's Corner
While the stock has a strong long-term trajectory with robust compounded profit growth and ROE improvement over the years, recent quarterly sales declines (-7% YoY) and revenue contraction (-8% TTM) signal deceleration on the top line despite sharp net profit outperformance (+144% YoY).
