US-Iran Ceasefire Triggers Global 'Risk-On' Pivot

  • Maintaining the repo rate at 5.25% provides much-needed cost-of-capital clarity for rate-sensitive sectors.

  • PSU banks with large G-Sec portfolios stand to benefit from the stabilization of bond yields.
