Market Summary

Daily Overview

  • The Indian stock market closed higher on September 12, 2025, with the Nifty 50 rising 108.50 points (0.43%) to 25,114, marking an eighth consecutive day of gains, the longest winning streak in over a year.
  • The BSE Sensex gained 355.97 points (0.44%) to close at 81,904.70, lifting overall market sentiment and recording a fifth straight day of rally.
  • Broad market optimism was supported by hopes for potential US Federal Reserve interest rate cuts following favorable US jobs data, offsetting concerns about inflation.
  • Retail investor sentiment remained positive, boosted by expectations of improving India-US trade relations and broad sectoral gains led by pharma, IT, auto, and financial sectors.
  • Despite overall gains, some sectors like FMCG, PSU banks, media, and real estate showed relative weakness, slightly tempering the broad market enthusiasm.

Sector Highlights

CPSE +1.04%

CPSE sector shows moderate strength supported by significant gains in select public sector enterprises such as Hindustan Copper, which surged over 6% driven by high trading volumes. However, PSU Banks showed weaker performance in contrast, reflecting a mixed outlook within the sector. Overall recovery in indices and positive primary issuance activity, such as Urban Company IPO, also buoy market sentiment for CPSE stocks.

Metal +0.93%

Metal sector is performing strongly, with notable buying interest in stocks like Hindalco and Hindustan Copper, contributing to sector gains. This strength is driven by prevailing positive sentiment in global commodity markets and favorable domestic factors, resulting in metal stocks gaining 0.5-1%. The rally is supported by easing inflation concerns and hopes of monetary easing by major central banks.

PSE +0.71%

Public Sector Enterprises (PSE) overall have a mixed performance. While some public enterprises such as Bharat Electronics and Shriram Finance are among the top gainers, the broader PSU Bank segment is under selling pressure. Investor optimism in select PSEs is fueled by government support and recovery in underlying business fundamentals, but cautious sentiments persist in banking PSEs.

Media -0.39%

Media sector is underperforming and facing selling pressure on September 12, 2025. The weakness is likely attributable to cautious investor sentiment amid broader market volatility and lack of sector-specific positive triggers. The sector closed lower while most other sectors showed gains, indicating short-term headwinds for media stocks.

FMCG -0.71%

FMCG sector is witnessing selling pressure despite the broader market recovery. Leading FMCG stocks like Hindustan Unilever (HUL) are among the top losers, reflecting concerns over inflationary pressures impacting raw material costs and potential margin compression. This cautious stance is limiting the sector's participation in the current market rally.

  • JSW Energy successfully added 317 MW of renewable energy capacity, bringing its total installed capacity to 13,097 MW.
