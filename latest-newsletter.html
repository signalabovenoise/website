Market Summary

Daily Overview

  • On 7 October 2025, the Indian stock market extended its winning streak with the BSE Sensex rising by 136 points (0.17%) to settle at 81,926, while NSE Nifty50 closed at 25,108.3, up 0.12%.
  • Broader market indices showed positive momentum, with Nifty Midcap 100 and Smallcap 100 gaining 0.47% and 0.31% respectively, indicating a broad-based rally beyond large-cap stocks.
  • Sector-wise, Realty led gains with a 1.09% rise, followed by Oil & Gas, Pharma, Consumer Durables, Healthcare, Bank, Auto, and Energy, while FMCG, PSU Banks, Media, Metal, and IT sectors faced selling pressure.
  • Retail investor sentiment remained cautiously positive with steady buying interest supported by heavyweights like Reliance Industries, ICICI Bank, HDFC Bank, and Bharti Airtel, suggesting confidence in blue-chip names.
  • Market gains occurred amid a stable domestic backdrop, balanced FII outflows and strong domestic institutional inflows, providing underpinning to the market despite mixed global cues.

Sector Highlights

Realty +1.09%

The Realty sector gained 1.09% supported by positive investor sentiment amid broader market gains, driven by strong buying in heavyweight sectors and expectations of stable demand in residential real estate. There were no negative policy announcements, and market participants focused on promising upcoming earnings and stable government land-related policies sustaining sector confidence.

Pharma +0.44%

Pharma sector rose 0.44% owing to optimistic outlook ahead of Q2 earnings season and favorable global developments such as reduced regulatory hurdles and stable export demand. Investor interest was buoyed by select healthcare stocks showing resilience, contributing to a moderate positive shift.

Auto +0.28%

The Auto sector increased by 0.28% primarily due to steady demand recovery signals in domestic auto sales, with some profit booking limiting gains. However, automotive stocks benefited from easing commodity input costs and anticipation of upcoming new model launches contributing to sectoral buying interest.

Media -0.46%

Media sector declined 0.46% due to profit booking pressures following recent gains and concerns over slowing ad revenue growth amid uncertain macroeconomic conditions. No significant positive policy or earnings triggers emerged for media stocks on the day, which weighed on investor sentiment.

FMCG -0.53%

FMCG stocks fell 0.53%, reflecting softness in consumer demand indicators and margin pressure from rising input costs. Additionally, cautious outlook ahead of earnings reports and weak daily consumption data contributed to negative sentiment in the sector.
