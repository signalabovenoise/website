Market Summary

Daily Overview

Indian benchmark indices ended the day in negative territory with Sensex declining by 0.95% and Nifty 50 falling by an equivalent margin. Broader indices like Nifty Smallcap 250 and Nifty Midcap 100 underperformed, losing 1.26% and 1.64% respectively. Weak earnings, geopolitical concerns, and foreign investor selling contributed to the bearish sentiment across the market. Small- and mid-cap segments, while showing resilience over the longer six-month horizon, struggled to maintain gains amid volatile global cues.
