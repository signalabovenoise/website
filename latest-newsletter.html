Market Summary

Daily Overview

  • The Indian stock market closed slightly lower on 23 September 2025 with the BSE Sensex ending down 57.87 points (-0.07%) at 82,102.10, amid broad-based profit booking across indices.

  • Energy stocks emerged as a standout performer, driven by positive sector-specific data, supporting the broader market resilience despite general weakness.
  • Overall market commentary highlighted a mixed scenario with investor cautiousness prevailing due to global uncertainties and domestic profit-booking pressures.

Sector Highlights

PSU Bank +1.09%

PSU Bank sector rose by +1.09% following strong buying support from Domestic Institutional Investors amid cautious market sentiment. The sector benefited from expectations of stable credit growth and improved asset quality, supported by government-led reforms and rising lending activity.

Metal +1.00%

The Metal sector gained +1.00% driven by recovering global commodity prices and sustained demand from infrastructure and industrial activity. Improved export prospects and easing concerns about trade disruptions also supported metal stocks.

Auto +0.62%

Auto sector increased +0.62% on the back of steady domestic demand supported by a normal monsoon and improving consumer sentiment. Positive quarterly earnings from key auto companies and supply chain normalization contributed to the sector's resilience.

Realty -0.89%

Realty sector declined -0.89% due to profit booking after recent gains, coupled with cautious investor stance amid rising interest rate concerns and sluggish sales data in select urban centers. Policy uncertainty around affordable housing subsidies may have further weighed on the sector.

FMCG -1.29%

FMCG sector fell -1.29%, pressured by input cost inflation affecting margins amid weak consumer discretionary spending signals. Additionally, market concerns over high raw material prices and a muted rural demand environment led to a sell-off in FMCG stocks.

Sharp Increase in H-1B Visa Costs Impacting IT Sector
  • The U.S. government's hike in H-1B visa costs raises operational expenses for Indian IT companies dependent on outsourcing talent to the U.S.
  • This geopolitical move may prompt Indian firms to pivot talent strategies toward onsite hiring or diversifying operations to non-U.S. geographies.
  • Technical sector stocks like Tech Mahindra, Infosys, and TCS faced margin pressures, leading to bearish sentiment in the short term.
Strong Gains in Banking Sector Led by IndusInd Bank and Axis Bank
  • Robust quarterly results and improved credit growth outlook fueled momentum for banking stocks.
  • Private banks are driving sector recovery, with asset quality improvements serving as key growth catalysts.
  • The bullish banking sentiment is likely to spill over into related sectors such as real estate and consumer durables.
Recovery in Indian Stock Indices Supported by Positive Global Cues
  • Sensex and Nifty rose modestly on signs of easing trade tensions and stronger foreign portfolio inflows.
  • Cyclical sectors like autos and infrastructure capitalized on improved global market sentiment to boost investor confidence.
  • The recovery reflects investor optimism toward India’s macroeconomic reforms and resilient growth fundamentals.

Emerging Opportunities

Energy sector continues to present compelling growth opportunities fueled by strong domestic demand and favorable government policies on renewable energy and infrastructure investments.
Non-ferrous metals, including copper and other non-copper metals, are emerging as strategic plays given supply constraints and growing demand in electric vehicles and semiconductor industries.
Public sector undertakings (PSUs), especially in banking and infrastructure, are being seen as undervalued assets poised for recovery due to ongoing reform measures.
Information technology offers selective opportunities in segments linked to emerging technologies despite recent sector correction, focusing on companies with strong innovation pipelines.
Construction and allied industries are favored for medium- to long-term investments given sustained government focus on infrastructure development and urbanization.
