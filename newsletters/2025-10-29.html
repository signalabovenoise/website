Market Summary

Daily Overview

  • On 29 October 2025, the Indian stock market closed on a strong note with the BSE Sensex rising by 369 points (0.44%) to 84,997 and the NSE Nifty crossing above the 26,000 mark, closing at 26,054, reflecting positive investor sentiment and resilience after recent volatility.
  • Market trading began with marginal gains, with the Sensex opening at 84,664 and Nifty at 25,982, supported by 41 of 50 Nifty companies showing gains and strong participation from institutional investors.
  • Retail investor sentiment appeared cautiously optimistic, buoyed by hopes of a US Federal Reserve rate cut and robust foreign institutional inflows exceeding Rs 10,000 crore, reinforcing confidence in the market’s near-term momentum.
  • The broader market breadth was positive as sectors such as media, metals, oil & gas-led gains offset weakness in the auto sector, indicating selective sector optimism while overall market mood remained constructive.
  • Key underlying themes influencing the market included profit-taking dynamics earlier in the week, liquidity strength led by both domestic and foreign institutional investors, and anticipation around global monetary policy decisions impacting risk appetite.

Sector Highlights

Energy +1.93%

The Energy sector rose as oil prices declined, leading to gains in shares of Indian Oil, HPCL, and BPCL, which climbed up to 6%. This move was tied directly to the global oil price trajectory on October 29, which improved downstream margins for these companies and boosted investor sentiment for refiners and distributors.

Commodities +1.73%

Commodities gained, fuelled by a positive global outlook on trade and easing global commodity pressures. There is no specific news solely on the commodities index, but the upward momentum aligns with positive movement in related sectors like metals and a general risk-on sentiment in emerging markets.

Metal +1.71%

The Metal sector advanced as the metal index hit a record high ahead of anticipated US-China trade talks. Stocks like SAIL and Hind Copper rose up to 8%. This rally appears tied to expectations of strong global demand for metals and speculation surrounding a potential easing of trade tensions, which would benefit Indian metal producers.

PSU Bank +0.02%

PSU Bank sector was nearly flat with a marginal gain. The absence of significant earnings reports or policy changes, combined with stable global rates and a lack of macro triggers, resulted in limited movement relative to other sectors.

Auto -0.73%

The Auto sector declined, underperforming broader indices. There were no major announcements or earnings from top automakers today, and the underperformance likely reflects sector rotation, with capital flowing towards high-performing sectors such as engineering and metals, and persistent concerns over consumer demand and input costs.

News TidBits
  • JSW Steel's stock rallied after the company reported a remarkable 307% year-on-year surge in its consolidated net profit for the second quarter.
  • The impressive earnings were driven by strong sales volumes and a significant, near-doubling of its exports, indicating robust international demand.
  • Favorable market conditions, including lower input costs and bullish brokerage outlooks, have created a positive tailwind for the entire steel sector.
  • The company's strong performance reflects the broader strength in the metals space, which is benefiting from global demand recovery and favorable domestic pricing.
  • For investors, this signals strong operational efficiency and market positioning, though future performance will remain linked to global commodity price cycles.

Top News Developments
Market Hits Near All-Time Highs on Global Cues and Trade Deal Hopes
  • The rally was primarily driven by optimism that the US Federal Reserve might signal a pause or cut in interest rates, which boosts liquidity for emerging markets like India.
  • Positive news about a potential US-India trade deal further fueled sentiment, as it promises to benefit export-oriented sectors and attract foreign investment.
  • This positive environment triggered a massive inflow from Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs), who made a single-day purchase of over $1 billion, reversing their recent selling trend.
  • The broad-based gains, with mid-cap and small-cap indices also rising, indicate high investor confidence across the market spectrum, not just in large-cap stocks.
SEBI Proposes Major Shake-Up in Mutual Fund Fees
  • SEBI's plan to cut the Total Expense Ratio (TER) and brokerage commissions directly targets the high costs faced by retail investors.
  • While beneficial for investors in the long run, the proposal immediately triggered a sharp sell-off in the stocks of Asset Management Companies (AMCs), who face a direct hit to their profitability.
  • This move reflects a broader regulatory push towards greater transparency and aligning the interests of fund houses with those of their investors.
  • For retail investors, this could lead to higher take-home returns from their mutual fund investments if the proposal is implemented.
Power, Ports, and Steel Sectors Lead Market Rally
  • Strong performance in sectors like power and ports is linked to the government's sustained push for infrastructure development, creating a long-term growth narrative.
  • Steel stocks like JSW Steel rallied on the back of stellar Q2 earnings, lower input costs, and a bullish outlook from brokerages, signaling strong corporate health.
  • The gains in power stocks like NTPC and Power Grid reflect investor confidence in India's energy transition and capacity expansion plans.
  • This sectoral rotation suggests that investors are moving capital into industries poised to benefit from both domestic economic activity and favorable global trade dynamics.
Strong Q2 Earnings Fuel Stock-Specific Moves
  • Corporate earnings are a key driver, as seen with JSW Steel's 307% profit surge which sent its stock soaring.
  • Conversely, Coal India's stock fell after it reported a 30% drop in quarterly profit, demonstrating how poor results are being punished by the market.
  • Varun Beverages' results showed a mixed picture with flat domestic volumes but strong international growth, highlighting diverse consumer demand trends.
  • These company-specific results provide retail investors with crucial insights into sectoral health, from robust industrial demand in steel to evolving consumption patterns in FMCG.

Emerging Opportunities

Electronics manufacturing received a significant policy boost with government approval of a ₹5,532 crore Electronics Manufacturing Scheme, positioning that sector for accelerated medium-term growth.
Infrastructure and maritime sectors are garnering investor attention, supported by Prime Minister Modi’s Mumbai visits emphasizing infrastructure vision, and the Maritime Leaders Conclave and Global Maritime CEO Forum highlighting long-term development potential.
Strong foreign institutional investor inflows and supportive domestic liquidity conditions create promising opportunities in sectors benefiting from stable capital availability, including banking, financial services, and insurance (BFSI).
Contrarian value selections emerge in select underperforming largecaps such as Mahindra & Mahindra and Bajaj Finance, given their price dips and potential rebound backed by broader market strength and sectoral tailwinds.
IPO market activity with offerings like Orkla India in the price band ₹695–730 presents fresh investment avenues reflecting growing investor participation and confidence in new economy companies.

Policy & Regulations

  • SEBI has proposed a significant overhaul of mutual fund fee structures, aiming to reduce the Total Expense Ratio (TER) and brokerage commissions charged to investors.
  • This regulatory proposal is designed to increase transparency and enhance returns for retail investors by lowering the cost of investing in mutual funds.
  • The announcement immediately impacted the market, causing shares of asset management companies (AMCs) like HDFC AMC and Nippon Life India to fall sharply on concerns that the new rules could significantly reduce their profitability.
