Market Summary

Daily Overview

  • The BSE Sensex rose by approximately 0.4% to close near 83,028 points on 18 September 2025, marking its third consecutive gain and reaching the highest level since early July.
  • The Nifty 50 index also advanced by about 0.3%, trading above 25,400, with small-cap and mid-cap stocks showing modest gains.
  • Market sentiment was buoyed by the US Federal Reserve's 25 basis points interest rate cut, alongside expectations of further rate reductions before the year-end, which is anticipated to support increased foreign inflows.
  • Optimism around US-India trade negotiations and the upcoming GST reform implementation on 22 September contributed positively to investor sentiment.
  • Overall, the market experienced broad green trading across sectors, led by technology, healthcare, and banking, reflecting a positive yet cautiously optimistic retail investor mood.

Sector Highlights

Pharma +1.50%

Pharma sector rose 1.50% driven by firm demand outlook amid easing regulatory pressures and positive earnings guidance from leading pharmaceutical companies, signaling continued growth potential.

IT +0.83%

IT sector gained 0.83% supported by upbeat quarterly results from major firms and strong global demand for digital transformation services, despite cautious overall market sentiment.

Bank +0.42%

Bank sector increased 0.42% as key banks including Axis Bank, SBI, and Kotak Bank reported strong earnings and sustained net interest margin improvement, amid overall positive sentiment in financial services.

Media -0.30%

Media sector declined 0.30% primarily due to profit booking after recent rallies and concerns over intensified competition and advertising revenue pressure from digital platforms.

Energy -0.35%

Energy sector fell 0.35% reflecting weak performance of major coal and oil companies affected by global commodity price softness and cautious demand forecasts, with Coal India Ltd notable among losers.
