Emerging Opportunities

IT/software services: Oversold at 15-18% below September 2024 peaks; rupee weakness becomes tailwind if dollar revenue contracts less than stock price decline—contrarian entry for 12-18 month hold.
PSU energy/oil refining: Near-term margin expansion from $105 crude offsets macro headwinds; dividend yields 7-9% cushion downside as government ownership provides stability anchor.
Domestic consumption plays (non-discretionary FMCG, telecom): Currency trauma irrelevant for rupee-denominated revenues; retail SIP fatigue created 2-3 year dislocation vs 6-7% real earnings growth.
Rupee bond/fixed income: At 95/USD, carry risk-reward inverted; corporate bond credit spreads widened 150bps—selective quality debt offers 7.5-8% yields with currency hedge embedded.
Unhedged exporters with pricing power (specialty chemicals, pharma APIs): Margin compression temporary; volume growth + price realization in FY27 as supply chains normalize post-crisis.

Policy & Regulations

  • The RBI has delivered a 25-basis-point repo rate cut to 6.25% while simultaneously expanding NDS-OM platform access to non-bank brokers to deepen liquidity in the government securities market.
  • A significant policy tailwind emerged for upstream energy producers like ONGC and Oil India as the government slashed offshore crude and natural gas royalties from 9.09% to 8%, likely boosting fair valuations by up to 11%.
  • SEBI is tightening market integrity by prohibiting the use of live market data for investor education and is in discussions to enable remote KYC for NRIs to broaden the foreign investor base.
  • Regulatory scrutiny on health-food claims and 'no added sugar' labels is increasing, potentially raising compliance costs for FMCG disruptors and specialized food brands.
  • Proposed gold-backed stablecoins are being evaluated as a mechanism to monetize India's massive idle gold reserves and decrease the financial system's reliance on dollar-linked assets.
