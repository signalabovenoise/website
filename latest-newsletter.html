Market Summary

Daily Overview

  • The Indian stock market extended its winning streak to six consecutive sessions on September 10, 2025, with the Sensex closing 324 points higher at 81,425 and the Nifty 50 rising 105 points to 24,973, narrowly missing the 25,000 milestone.
  • Retail investor sentiment remained bullish at market close, supported by optimism around a potential India-US trade deal resolution.
  • Broader markets outperformed the headline indices, with the Nifty Midcap and Smallcap indices gaining 0.9% and 0.7%, respectively.
  • Sector-wise, IT stocks led the gains while auto, media, and consumer durables saw profit booking pressure.
  • The market rally was underpinned by positive macro sentiment and anticipation of policy clarity, while cautious profit-taking prevented sharper upside beyond key psychological levels.

Sector Highlights

IT +2.63%

The IT sector is performing strongly, with the IT index up 2.5% on September 10, 2025, driven by optimism over ongoing India-US trade negotiations and expectations of a Federal Reserve interest rate cut next week. Key IT stocks such as HCL Tech, TCS, Outlook Financial Services, and Wipro have rallied due to hopes of revived technology spending and stronger H2FY26 earnings. Investor sentiment is buoyed by GST rationalisation and monetary easing benefits supporting valuations in this sector.

PSU Bank +2.09%

PSU Banks saw significant gains with the PSU Bank index up 2.3%. The rally is attributed to renewed optimism from India-US trade talks that are positively affecting market sentiment. Stocks such as Kotak Mahindra Bank and HDFC Bank were among the active gainers, supported by expectations of better credit growth and improved asset quality amid easing monetary conditions.

CPSE +1.18%

The CPSE (Central Public Sector Enterprises) and PSU indices also experienced positive movement, with the BSE PSU index up 1%. Top gainers in this category included Bharat Dynamics, NMDC Steel, and Engineers India, benefiting from rising infra and defense sector optimism linked to government spending and policy support.

Media -0.64%

The media sector showed weak performance today, with the Nifty Media index down by approximately 0.14% to 0.18% lower. Selling pressure affected media stocks amid sector rotation and investor caution. Despite some buying interest earlier, the overall trend for the media sector is negative compared to other sectors.

Auto -1.28%

The auto sector is underperforming, with the auto index down about 0.8%. Major auto manufacturers such as M&M, Maruti Suzuki, and Tata Motors were among the top losers. Weakness in the sector is attributed to concerns over demand slowdown, inflationary pressures, and global supply chain uncertainties impacting production and sales.
