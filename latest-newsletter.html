Market Summary

Daily Overview

  • On 2 September 2025, the Indian stock market saw mixed activity with benchmark indices showing intra-day volatility; the NSE Nifty 50 initially rose by about 0.81% reaching near 24,635 but later closed slightly lower below 24,600, while the BSE Sensex also fluctuated ending modestly down by around 206 points to 80,157.
  • Retail investor sentiment on the day remained neutral overall, with broader market segments like Midcaps and Smallcaps showing marginal gains despite the large-cap indices closing weaker.
  • Foreign portfolio investors (FPIs) continued to be net sellers for a sixth straight session, offloading stocks worth approximately Rs 1,703 crore, whereas domestic institutional investors (DIIs) remained net buyers, supporting the market.
  • Market volatility showed an uptick, evidenced by a rise in the India VIX to around 3%, indicating increased uncertainty among investors.
  • The day was influenced by macro events such as Prime Minister Narendra Modi inaugurating the Semicon India 2025 conference and policy updates related to equity derivatives aimed at improving market fairness after scrutiny of high-frequency trading strategies.
