Sector Highlights

IT +2.76%

On September 9, 2025, the IT sector showed a strong performance, with the Nifty IT index rising 3% and snapping a five-day losing streak. Gains were led by major players like Infosys and Wipro. Key drivers include Infosys's announcement of a share buyback proposal scheduled for September 11, investor optimism on steady earnings, and expectations of strong deal pipelines despite global trade tensions. The sector benefited from a recovery in market sentiment after August's volatility due to US tariffs and FII selling, supported by strong domestic fundamentals and positive outlooks on digital spending.

Pharma +0.86%

The Pharma sector gained strength on September 9, 2025, supported by strong performances in companies like Glenmark, which saw a share price boost following its subsidiary securing $700 million from NYSE-listed AbbVie in a licensing deal. This deal, along with broad-based gains across the sector earlier in the week, highlights ongoing investor confidence driven by robust global demand for pharmaceuticals and strategic partnerships, despite macroeconomic headwinds.

FMCG +0.58%

The FMCG sector experienced weakness on September 9, 2025, with consumer-facing stocks such as Trent and Eternal declining by up to 2% due to profit booking after recent gains linked to the impending GST 2.0 tax overhaul. While the sector has stable long-term demand, short-term pressure is attributed to investors taking profits amid uncertainty about the details and impact of the new GST regime on consumption and margins.

PSE -0.19%

Public Sector Enterprises (PSE) saw moderate gains of around 1.7% during the week ending September 5, 2025, continuing a steady uptrend. The sector's performance is bolstered by government reforms, capital infusion, and expectations of improved efficiency. However, Foreign Portfolio Investors remained net sellers, influenced by global tariff tensions, causing some caution despite domestic support from institutional buying.

Realty -0.30%

The Realty sector showed mixed performance on September 9, 2025, with shares such as Sunteck Realty experiencing profit booking after a sharp 16% rise over two sessions. Overall, the sector faces short-term volatility as investors reassess valuations following recent strong gains, amid stable demand from affordable housing segments and improving urban infrastructure, but tempered by concerns over interest rate outlook and upcoming policy shifts.
