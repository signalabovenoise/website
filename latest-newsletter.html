Market Summary

Daily Overview

  • Indian stock market opened weak on 22 September 2025, pressured mainly by declines in IT stocks, leading to a broad-based slowdown in indices such as Sensex and Nifty50.
  • Sensex slipped approximately 466 points, reflecting investor caution amid global and domestic headwinds, including new U.S. visa regulations impacting Indian tech companies.
  • Retail investor sentiment remained cautious but with an underlying positive bias due to continuing foreign institutional investor (FII) buying trends observed over the prior week.
  • The Indian rupee showed slight strength relative to previous sessions, providing some support to the overall market outlook amid the mixed economic signals.
  • Major overall news influencing the market included continuing regulatory changes abroad, mainly pertaining to U.S. visa rules affecting IT sector margins and calls by leadership promoting domestic industry themes.

Sector Highlights

Commodities +0.69%

Commodities rose 0.69% driven by a new record high in gold prices on MCX at Rs 1.11 lakh/10 grams amid safe-haven demand and inflation concerns, supporting metals and precious metals segments.

Energy +0.69%

Energy sector gained 0.69%, led by strong stock performance such as Adani Power jumping 18-20% after a stock split announcement, boosting investor sentiment in power and energy stocks.

Media +0.48%

Media sector increased by 0.48% supported by ongoing festival season advertising uptick starting with Navratri, boosting demand for media and entertainment advertising revenues.

Pharma -1.41%

Pharma declined 1.41% due to negative sentiment from proposed tariff legislation by US Senators targeting Indian shrimp exports, causing concerns about trade barriers impacting sector supply chains.

IT -2.95%

IT sector dropped 2.95% sharply after US administration raised H-1B visa application fees for new applicants to $100,000, escalating costs for outsourcing firms and impacting India-origin tech workers and firms.

Top News Developments
New U.S. Visa Rules Impacting Indian IT Stocks
  • The U.S. has introduced tighter visa restrictions, increasing costs for Indian IT companies employing H-1B and other visa holders, compressing margins.
  • Geopolitical tensions are prompting Indian IT firms to rethink staffing models, influencing future CAPEX and automation strategies.
  • Major IT firms like Tech Mahindra and Infosys are facing bearish sentiment in the short term due to cost pressures impacting profitability.
PM Modi’s Renewed ‘Swadeshi’ Call and Market Implications
  • The government’s emphasis on domestic manufacturing aims to boost import substitution and improve India’s trade balance.
  • Potential policy incentives for electronics, defense, and renewable sectors could accelerate R&D investments and enhance Make-in-India initiatives.
  • Capital goods and domestic manufacturers like Larsen & Toubro may experience medium-term inflows driven by localization efforts.
IndusInd Bank Appoints Viral Damania as CFO
  • CFO changes in major banks suggest upcoming strategic shifts in capital allocation and regulatory compliance.
  • Investor sentiment could be mixed initially, signaling shifts in private banking strategies and long-term profitability expectations.
  • IndusInd Bank's stock performance in the coming quarters will closely reflect any operational shifts announced under new leadership.
