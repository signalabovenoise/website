Fundamentals Corner
The company's long-term growth engine is hitting a significant bottleneck, with TTM sales and profit growth plummeting to mid-single digits compared to their robust 17-24% five-year averages.
While a stable 17% ROE indicates the core business model remains intact, recent quarterly data reveals a sharp margin squeeze as annual expenses (+11.2%) are growing four times faster than revenue (+2.5%).
The modest 17.8 P/E and negative one-year stock return suggest the market has already priced in this slowdown, offering a potential entry point for patient investors if the recent 16% QoQ profit rebound signals a sustainable turnaround.
