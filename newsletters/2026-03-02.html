Sector Highlights

Metal +0.24%

Metals stood out as a rare pocket of resilience amid broad-based risk-off sentiment. The sector's modest positive performance (+0.24%) likely reflects safe-haven demand and potential upside from geopolitical tensions driving commodity prices higher, contrasting with weakness in cyclical and rate-sensitive segments.

Pharma +0.02%

Pharma remained essentially flat (+0.02%) despite broader market weakness. The sector showed relative stability as investors rotated away from cyclicals, though it did not benefit significantly from typical defensive safe-haven flows, suggesting mixed sentiment around pharmaceutical valuations amid global uncertainty.

CPSE -0.17%

Central Public Sector Enterprises declined marginally (-0.17%) as part of the broad-based selloff. Energy sector weakness—which plummeted 20.58% due to rising geopolitical tensions in West Asia and crude oil price surges—weighed heavily on CPSE indices, as many state-owned enterprises operate in energy and commodities-exposed segments.

PSU Bank -1.84%

PSU Bank stocks fell 1.84% amid selling pressure on banking counters driven by concerns over rising bond yields and margin compression in a risk-off environment. Banking stocks, carrying significant weight in benchmark indices, faced broad selling as investors adopted a defensive stance following geopolitical escalation in West Asia.

Auto -2.20%

Auto sector declined 2.20% as cyclical and rate-sensitive stocks came under pressure amid global uncertainty. The sector faced additional headwinds from rising crude oil prices, which increase input costs for vehicle manufacturers and fuel-linked expenses, alongside weakened consumer demand sentiment triggered by geopolitical tensions and Middle East conflict escalation.
