Sector Highlights

CPSE +0.05%

CPSE showed minimal movement (+0.05%), reflecting a balanced market sentiment. While PSU banks within the broader CPSE umbrella faced selling pressure due to global financial stability concerns and profit booking, this was likely offset by strength in other PSU sectors. The sector remained relatively stable amid broader market volatility driven by geopolitical tensions and crude oil price fluctuations.

IT -0.44%

IT stocks declined marginally (-0.44%) but demonstrated relative resilience compared to other sectors. The weakness was offset by fundamental support from rupee depreciation, as IT companies earn significant revenue in dollars and benefit from a weaker rupee. Specific IT stocks like TCS (-0.87%), TECHM (-2.04%), and BEL (-0.28%) faced selling pressure amid broader market corrections, though the sector's dollar-earning advantage limited overall downside.

Pharma -0.50%

Pharma declined modestly (-0.50%) but remained relatively stable as a defensive sector. Pharma acts as a safe haven during market uncertainty and geopolitical tensions. The sector's mild decline reflects selective profit-taking rather than fundamental weakness, with defensive positioning protecting it from steeper losses seen in cyclical sectors.

Realty -3.17%

Realty experienced a significant decline (-3.17%), making it one of the worst-performing sectors. The weakness reflects selling pressure in cyclical sectors amid global uncertainty, U.S. market corrections, and broader Asian market softness. Realty, being highly sensitive to interest rates, credit conditions, and global investor sentiment, faced heightened pressure as international cues turned cautious and geopolitical tensions persisted.

PSU Bank -3.86%

PSU Bank declined sharply (-3.86%), facing considerable selling pressure driven by profit booking and concerns over global financial stability. Both PSU and private banks were affected by broader banking sector weakness amid macro concerns, including the Indian rupee hovering near record lows and uncertain global cues. Bank Nifty showed weakness despite recent recovery attempts, with consolidation expected in the 51,400-54,800 range.
