Market Summary

Daily Overview

  • On September 29, 2025, the Indian stock market showed a mostly flat to slight recovery trend, with Sensex and Nifty indices moving up and down during the session but closing near previous levels.
  • Nifty crossed the 24,700 mark intraday, indicating some recovery after a series of declines, though the overall market sentiment remained cautious.
  • Retail investor sentiment was subdued due to pressure on key sectors such as IT and pharma, which faced significant downturns leading to psychological resistance in re-entry at previous high price levels.
  • Broader markets outperformed the benchmark indices despite the Sensex and Nifty falling for a seventh consecutive session, reflecting selective confidence in some mid and small-cap stocks.
  • Overall market sentiment was influenced by macroeconomic factors including government fiscal targets and supply chain disruptions like GST rate cuts impacting consumer staples sales.

Sector Highlights

PSU Bank +1.78%

PSU Bank stocks rose 1.78% driven by positive earnings reports and SBI’s supportive views on a potential RBI rate cut, which may ease borrowing costs and boost credit growth in the sector.

PSE +1.17%

The Public Sector Enterprises (PSE) index gained 1.17% supported by government initiatives enhancing growth prospects, including increased agricultural trade partnerships with Russia and steady demand in key public enterprises.

Realty +0.88%

Realty sector rose 0.88% due to Maharashtra's housing push announced recently, signaling increased demand and government support for residential construction, improving sector outlook.

Auto -0.18%

Auto sector declined slightly by 0.18%, pressured by global supply chain disruptions affecting components availability and cautious consumer demand sentiments amid broader market volatility.

Media -0.85%

Media sector dropped 0.85%, impacted by global tariff uncertainties linked to US-India trade tensions, notably new pharma tariffs affecting Indian companies, which have indirect negative sentiment spillover on media stocks.

Top News Developments
Tata Motors Leadership Reshuffle: New CEO & CFO Announced
  • Shailesh Chandra and Dhiman Gupta have been appointed as CEO & MD and CFO respectively, signaling strategic leadership changes.
  • The move is expected to bolster Tata Motors’ adaptability to EV transitions and strengthen its fiscal operations.
  • This announcement brings renewed optimism for automotive and related ancillary sectors, such as batteries and semiconductors.
  • EV and hybrid vehicle momentum will likely accelerate, driving investment in infrastructure-linked stocks.
  • Retail investors should monitor auto manufacturers for medium-term growth opportunities tied to leadership-driven operational improvements.
HUL Q2 Sales Slow as GST Rate Cuts Create Temporary Impact
  • Near-flat growth in Hindustan Unilever's Q2 revenue is attributed to the short-term effects of GST rate reductions in September.
  • The disruptions in sales recognition suggest strong demand fundamentals with temporary distortions rather than structural weaknesses.
  • The FMCG sector may face transient challenges, but normalization is expected as policy-driven price benefits filter through.
  • Investors should note volatility in FMCG earnings forecasting cycles while considering long-term consumption trends for entry points.
  • HUL and peers like ITC remain resilient picks as affordability-driven demand recovery kicks in post-Q3 FY26.
DEA Secretary Confident of FY26 Fiscal Targets Amid Global Pressures
  • India's government expressed confidence in meeting fiscal deficit and growth targets despite inflationary and geopolitical challenges.
  • Stable policy frameworks for revenue mobilization and disinvestment reinforce macroeconomic stability, providing a positive outlook for interest-sensitive sectors.
  • Bond markets are likely to stabilize as fiscal risk perceptions decrease, making debt instruments attractive in the near term.
  • Retail investors should focus on banking, NBFCs, and infra-linked plays for opportunities in a predictable policy environment.
  • FII sentiment might improve in the medium term, aiding market liquidity and confidence.
