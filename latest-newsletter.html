Top News Developments
FII Outflows Trigger Major Market Correction as Benchmarks Plunge
  • Persistent selling by Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) has driven a significant market downturn, with Sensex and Nifty falling over 1% due to their substantial offloading of equities.
  • These FII outflows, observed since early 2026, reflect growing caution over global trade-war rhetoric, disappointing earnings from Indian heavyweights, and concerns regarding elevated market valuations.
  • While Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) provided strong buying support, their efforts were insufficient to counter the FII pressure, signaling a potential liquidity crunch, especially in mid and smallcap segments.
  • The broad-based selling primarily impacted sectors like IT, financials, and metals, with specific stocks like Wipro and Reliance Industries witnessing significant declines.
  • This trend points towards a bearish outlook for Indian markets over the coming weeks, with potential for further downside if global reversal sentiments persist, though short-term rebounds are possible if DII buying strengthens.
Adani Power Secures NCLAT Approval for ₹4,000 Crore Resolution Plan
  • The National Company Law Appellate Tribunal (NCLAT) has upheld Adani Power's ₹4,000 crore resolution plan for Vidarbha Industries Power Limited, providing a crucial legal win.
  • This clears a major legal overhang, enabling Adani Power to unlock assets and continue its aggressive expansion within the power sector, aligning with India's push for energy security.
  • The decision improves the capital efficiency for distressed asset plays and could encourage further investment and easier funding access across the broader power sector.
  • The development has direct positive implications for Adani Power, and indirectly for its group companies like Adani Ports, which was noted as a gainer despite the broader market decline.
  • A bullish outlook is expected for Adani Power over the coming quarters due to anticipated earnings accretion, with immediate news-driven trading momentum.
GMDC Boosts India's Rare Earth Self-Reliance with BARC Technology
  • Gujarat Mineral Development Corporation (GMDC) is advancing its rare earth production capabilities utilizing indigenous technology developed by the Bhabha Atomic Research Centre (BARC).
  • This strategic move is crucial for India's self-reliance (Atmanirbhar Bharat) in critical minerals, aiming to reduce dependence on foreign sources, particularly China, for essential components in EVs and defense.
  • The initiative strengthens domestic supply chains for electric vehicles (EVs) and production-linked incentive (PLI) schemes, potentially attracting further investment into manufacturing.
  • The development is expected to provide policy tailwinds for mining reforms and enhance the liquidity of Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs) through strategic asset monetization.
  • This represents a long-term bullish theme for GMDC and related mining/EV sectors, with an initial sentiment pop expected in the short term.
