Sector Highlights

CPSE +1.24%

Central Public Sector Enterprises gained 1.24% driven by strong performance in energy and infrastructure-related PSUs. The power, industrials, and utilities sectors hit new 52-week highs, reflecting robust investor interest in infrastructure and energy themes that benefited CPSE constituents.

Energy +1.22%

Energy sector advanced 1.22% with the Oil & Gas index rising 1% and energy index gaining 1.6%. Top gainers included ONGC and Coal India, supported by commodity price stability and infrastructure-focused investor demand amid ongoing global macro uncertainties.

PSE +0.72%

Power, Utilities, and related public sector enterprises gained 0.72%, with the S&P BSE Power and Utilities indices reaching new 52-week highs. This reflected sustained investor confidence in infrastructure and energy transition themes, offsetting broader market weakness.

Bank -1.54%

Banking sector declined 1.54%, with both PSU and private banks under pressure. The Private Bank index fell 0.8% alongside the broader PSU bank weakness, driven by concerns over asset quality and earnings prospects ahead of earnings announcements.

PSU Bank -2.15%

PSU Bank sector fell 2.15%, the top laggard among 38 tracked sectors. Weakness was driven by asset quality concerns and uncertain earnings prospects. Major declines included Canara Bank (-2.14%) and Bank of India (-2.95%), contributing significantly to overall financial segment underperformance.
