Sector Highlights

Auto +1.93%

Auto sector gained +1.93% driven by crude oil falling below $100 following US-Iran deal progress. Lower oil prices reduce input costs for automotive manufacturers and improve consumer purchasing power for vehicle acquisition. The sector was identified as one of the primary beneficiaries of the geopolitical de-escalation and commodity repricing.

Energy +0.82%

Energy sector rose +0.82% despite WTI crude plummeting below $93. Oil marketing companies (OMCs) like BPCL and HPCL showed strength as the sector was repriced by falling crude. While lower oil prices typically pressure energy companies, the intraday trading focus shifted to OMCs capitalizing on downstream margins improvement at lower input costs.

Realty +0.63%

Realty sector gained +0.63% amid broad market recovery. The sector benefited from the constructive macro backdrop created by FII turning net buyers (Rs 2,835.62 crore inflows in May) and simultaneous DII buying (Rs 4,764.16 crore), signaling structural institutional demand. Nifty 50 closing above 24,300 resistance created positive momentum across real estate stocks.

FMCG -0.76%

FMCG sector declined -0.76% despite overall market gains. Sector faced selling pressure on Wednesday and maintained weakness on Thursday. This underperformance reflects selective market rotation away from defensive FMCG stocks toward cyclical sectors (auto, energy, metals) benefiting directly from crude oil decline and geopolitical risk reduction.

IT -0.77%

IT sector fell -0.77% amid mixed sectoral performance. The sector underperformed as institutional flows and market momentum favored cyclical sectors directly repriced by crude oil collapse and improved risk sentiment. IT stocks, lacking direct commodity tailwinds or earnings catalysts from the US-Iran deal, faced relative weakness in the selective buying environment.

Top News Developments
Brent Crude Crashes Below $100 on US-Iran MoU Framework
  • A potential formalization of the US-Iran MoU has triggered a 7% drop in Brent crude, easing India's massive $120B annual import bill and core inflation concerns.
  • Lower fuel costs act as a massive tailwind for OMCs like BPCL and HPCL, while input cost relief boosts margins for paint and chemical giants like Asian Paints.
  • This geopolitical shift has encouraged FIIs to flip to net buyers, signaling a structural rotation back into Indian risk-on assets and cyclicals.
  • Sustained crude prices below $100 could provide the RBI with the necessary room to accelerate interest rate cuts in late FY26.
Q4 FY26 Earnings Reveal Capex Revival and Manufacturing Strength
  • Key earnings beats from CG Power (+32% profit) and Polycab (27% revenue growth) signal a robust revival in India's industrial and infrastructure cycles.
  • The Services PMI hitting 58.8 validates strong domestic demand and provides a fundamental floor for valuations despite global macroeconomic uncertainty.
  • MRF and Hero MotoCorp's resilient performance highlights that premiumization and replacement demand are successfully offsetting rising rubber and metal costs.
  • Strategic pivots toward defense and data-center exports by firms like Bharat Forge are creating new high-growth sub-themes for retail portfolios.
Banking Consolidation and Profitability Shifts in Digital Finance
  • Kotak Mahindra Bank’s stake acquisitions in Federal Bank and AU Small Finance Bank mark a major consolidation trend in the private banking landscape.
  • Paytm (One97) reaching a financial turning point with its fourth consecutive profitable quarter signals a shift toward sustainable margins in the fintech space.
  • Higher 'other income' and operational leverage are helping mid-tier banks like Karur Vysya offset tighter regulatory provisioning requirements.
  • Wealth managers are identifying private banks as a 'value opportunity' after significant underperformance relative to the overextended renewable energy sector.
Retail and Consumer Slowdown Impacts Discretionary Spending Giants
  • Titan and Whirlpool's steep declines highlight how tighter credit conditions and inflation are denting high-end consumer financing and housing activity.
  • A 17% plunge in Whirlpool India serves as a sector-wide warning for consumer durables facing dividend suspensions and outlook cuts.
  • Tata Motors' earnings miss in the commercial vehicle segment reflects a softening in domestic logistics demand and rising input cost pressures.
  • Investors are increasingly being cautioned against 'blind accumulation' of stocks with high institutional holdings, as many have still corrected over 25%.
