Sector Highlights

PSU Bank +1.67%

PSU Bank stocks rose 1.67% primarily due to positive earnings reports from major PSU banks such as State Bank of India, which showed solid quarterly results and improved asset quality. Additionally, supportive macroeconomic cues and increased foreign institutional investor (FII) buying in the banking sector helped uplift PSU bank shares.

Realty +1.67%

The Realty sector gained 1.67% following strong buying interest in key developers like Godrej Properties and Brigade, driven by government measures encouraging affordable housing and improving demand outlook. Additionally, positive news on government infrastructure spending and increased home loan approvals supported the sector's upward movement.

Pharma +1.29%

Pharma stocks advanced 1.29% after the Delhi High Court allowed Natco Pharma to sell the drug 'Risdiplam' in India, boosting market sentiment. Further, robust Q2 earnings from leading pharma companies and cautious optimism on easing regulatory pressures added to sector strength.

IT -0.05%

IT sector declined marginally by 0.05% despite Q2 earnings announcements from major players like TCS. The subdued performance was attributed to cautious global demand projections and some profit-taking after recent gains, with investors awaiting clearer guidance on margin outlook amid macro uncertainties.

Metal -0.91%

Metal sector fell 0.91%, led by declines in Tata Steel and JSW Steel, which dropped over 1%. The sector faced pressure due to weak global commodity prices, incremental supply concerns, and recent reduction in China’s steel demand outlook. Domestic order inflows showed limited improvement, failing to offset bearish commodity sentiment.

Top News Developments
Market Hits One-Month High as Rally Broadens
  • The Sensex and Nifty rallied to their highest levels in nearly a month, driven by a clear sector rotation into pharmaceuticals and financials.
  • This upmove was critically supported by the return of Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPIs), who turned net buyers, signaling renewed global confidence in the Indian market.
  • The rally is becoming more broad-based, with the outperforming auto sector also contributing significantly, which suggests a healthy risk appetite among investors.
  • Consistent buying by Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) has provided a strong foundation, preventing sharp declines and supporting the market's upward trajectory.
IT Sector at a Crossroads: TCS's AI Pivot vs. Weak Earnings
  • TCS delivered Q2 results that missed market expectations, raising concerns about near-term demand and causing its stock to fall.
  • Simultaneously, the company announced a bold $6 billion investment in an AI data centre business, a strategic pivot aiming for future growth but requiring significant capital.
  • This shift is causing major internal changes, evidenced by a headcount reduction of nearly 20,000, as the company reallocates resources towards AI.
  • The news created a split reaction in the market: while TCS and other IT stocks faced pressure, group company Tata Communications surged as a likely beneficiary of the new infrastructure plan.
IPO Frenzy Peaks with LG's Record-Breaking Demand
  • The IPO for LG Electronics India was oversubscribed 54 times, attracting bids worth ₹4.4 lakh crore and signaling massive investor appetite for quality manufacturing companies.
  • This success validates the government's Production-Linked Incentive (PLI) schemes and is expected to encourage other multinational companies to list their Indian arms.
  • The overwhelming demand for LG contrasts sharply with the muted listing of WeWork India, showing that investors are discerning and prioritize strong fundamentals and profitability over speculative growth stories.
  • The positive sentiment is likely to boost upcoming and ongoing IPOs, creating a favorable environment for companies in the primary market.
Retail Investors Flock to Gold Amid Rupee Weakness
  • Record-high gold prices are driving a surge in retail investment, with record inflows of over ₹8,500 crore into gold ETFs this month.
  • This flight to safety is happening as the Indian Rupee trades near its all-time low against the US dollar, prompting investors to hedge against currency depreciation.
  • The trend is directly benefiting gold loan NBFCs like Muthoot Finance and Manappuram Finance, as higher gold prices increase their loan portfolio value and attract more borrowers.
  • India's household wealth in gold has now hit a staggering $3.8 trillion, underscoring the metal's deep-rooted importance as a store of value for Indian investors.
