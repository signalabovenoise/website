Market Summary

Daily Overview

  • The Indian stock market showed a mixed to slightly positive close on 5 September 2025, with the NSE Nifty 50 ending marginally higher by 0.08% at 24,734 and the BSE Sensex gaining 0.19% to 80,718, despite some intra-day volatility and profit booking at higher levels near 25,000 in the Nifty 50 index.
  • Market sentiment was cautiously optimistic, supported by positive consumer consumption sentiment driven by the government’s GST restructuring that reduced most goods tax rates to 5% and 18%, effective from 22 September, boosting hopes for economic growth.
  • Retail investors exhibited a restrained trading mood, with indexes consolidating in a range of 24,400 to 25,000 and investors advised to maintain strict stop losses given sideways movement and stock-specific volatility.
  • Key sectors that influenced the market included auto and metals which performed well, while FMCG and IT sectors saw some drag, reflecting sector rotation and selective buying interests rather than broad-based market rallies.
  • Overall market commentary indicated a phase of consolidation and selective buying amid positive policy changes and macroeconomic reforms, with expectations for the indices to test resistance near 25,200 levels if bullish momentum sustains.

Top News Developments
RBI cancels September 5 bank holiday; markets operational.
  • The RBI announced that the previously scheduled September 5 bank holiday is cancelled, ensuring uninterrupted market operations.
  • This decision minimizes disruption, particularly for government securities and rupee interest rate derivatives markets.
  • Delayed settlement timelines on September 8 may induce short-term volatility in liquidity and funding markets.
  • Banking stocks like SBI and HDFC Bank could see slight impacts depending on short-term interest rate adjustments.
Flat equity performance as sectors diverge; auto stocks rise, IT falls.
  • Sensex remained flat while Nifty gained slightly, driven by strong performances in auto and metal sectors.
  • Auto gains signal industry optimism following GST tax cuts boosting vehicle affordability.
  • IT and FMCG sectors faced profit booking, highlighting cautious investor sentiment in defensive plays.
  • Key impacted stocks include M&M, Maruti Suzuki (gainers) and ITC, HCL Tech (laggards).
Strong HSBC Services PMI supports economic resilience.
  • India’s HSBC Services PMI at 65.2 reflects robust growth in service activity, driven by strong domestic consumption.
  • Stable money supply growth at 9.8% indicates consistent liquidity, supporting accommodative credit policies.
  • Macro resilience places financial stocks and sectors linked to domestic consumption in favorable positions.
  • RBI policy decisions could lean towards stability in the near term, aiding market confidence.

Emerging Opportunities
The recent GST reforms creating lower tax slabs at 5% and 18% create promising growth opportunities for consumer goods sectors poised to benefit from increased consumption.
Auto and metals sectors showed relative strength and remain attractive for medium to long-term investors anticipating cyclical upturns due to infrastructure growth and industrial recovery.
Financial services and banking remain a focus given the positive outlook on credit growth and ongoing economic reforms aimed at improving financial inclusion and digitization.
Certain companies like Adani Ports and Jayaswal Neco Industries have been identified by research houses as compelling investment prospects on 5 September 2025, reflecting selective stock-specific opportunities within industrial and infrastructure themes.
Contrarian opportunities may arise in IT and FMCG stocks due to recent profit booking, offering potential entry points for value investors willing to capitalize on sector rotations.
