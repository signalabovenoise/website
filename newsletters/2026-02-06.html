Sector Highlights

FMCG +2.27%

FMCG gained 2.27%, driven by strong performance in HUL, which was among the top gainers of the session. The sector benefited from positive sentiment following the RBI's decision to maintain the repo rate at 5.25%, which supports consumer spending and reduces financing costs for FMCG companies.

MNC +1.04%

MNC gained 1.04%, with Bharti Airtel listed among top gainers. The modest positive performance reflects the broader market recovery on the day, though the sector faced headwinds from weak global cues, particularly sharp declines in US technology stocks overnight.

Realty +0.63%

Realty gained 0.63%, supported by the RBI's unchanged repo rate at 5.25%, which maintains favorable borrowing conditions for real estate developers and homebuyers. The sector's modest gain reflects cautious sentiment ahead of the RBI monetary policy announcement.

Pharma -0.72%

Pharma declined 0.72%, underperforming the broader market recovery. The sector faced selling pressure amid weak global cues from US markets, where technology stocks experienced sharp declines overnight, creating a risk-off sentiment that affected growth-oriented sectors.

IT -1.47%

IT declined 1.47%, with TCS and Tech Mahindra among the top losers of the session. The sector was significantly impacted by a sharp sell-off on Wall Street, where the Nasdaq fell 1.59% to its lowest level since November, driven by heavy losses in major technology stocks. This global weakness directly weighed on Indian IT exporters.

Top News Developments
RBI's Neutral Stance & Benign Inflation Outlook
  • Maintaining the repo rate at 5.25% signals that the RBI is prioritizing economic stability while shifting toward proactive liquidity management over aggressive tightening.
  • The reduction of the FY26 inflation forecast to 2.1% creates a 'Goldilocks' environment that could eventually trigger rate cuts if domestic growth momentum softens.
  • This policy stability specifically benefits rate-sensitive sectors like Banking and Realty, while helping blue-chip lenders maintain steady asset quality despite rising deposit costs.
US-India Trade Pact as a Macro Catalyst
  • The structural reset in trade relations resolves long-standing tariff overhangs, particularly benefiting the Indian IT and manufacturing sectors within the 'China+1' strategy.
  • By strengthening the Rupee and reducing geopolitical risk premiums, the deal helps offset domestic liquidity pressures caused by recent Securities Transaction Tax (STT) hikes.
  • The pact encourages supply chain localization, which is already reflected in the record order backlogs of industrial players like Hitachi Energy and Godrej Properties.
The AI Valuation Crisis and Sectoral Divergence
  • Indian IT majors are facing a valuation squeeze as investors fear that generative AI will cannibalize traditional labor-intensive migration models.
  • The upcoming Fractal Analytics IPO is a pivotal moment that will determine if the market is ready to pivot from traditional services to high-margin AI pure-plays.
  • Analysts recommend rotating portfolios toward 'AI-resilient' sectors like defense and textiles, where core business models are less vulnerable to immediate automation risks.
Corporate Earnings Reveal Stark Sectoral Splits
  • The automotive sector is diverging, with Hero MotoCorp and MRF showing volume-driven strength while Tata Motors struggles with JLR's EBITDA plunge and EV hurdles.
  • Real estate and organized retail continue to gain market share, as evidenced by Godrej Properties' record sales and the robust profit jumps seen in Kalyan Jewellers.
  • Infrastructure and EPC players like KNR Constructions face execution risks from rising costs, suggesting a need for investors to focus on firms with superior operating leverage.

Policy & Regulations

  • The RBI is proposing a restricted Euroclear bond-settlement link that prioritizes long-term sovereign wealth funds while excluding FIIs to prevent sudden capital flight and maintain currency stability.
  • SEBI has moved to curb excessive derivative volatility by withdrawing calendar spread margin benefits on expiry days, a move that coincides with an upcoming STT hike that will double trading costs for futures.
  • New RBI proposals to cap customer liability for small-value digital payment frauds at ₹25,000 aim to enhance retail user trust while potentially increasing compliance costs for UPI platforms.
  • The government's plan to monetize public sector assets via REITs, combined with lower interest rates, is creating a high-quality pipeline for yield-seeking commercial real estate investors.
