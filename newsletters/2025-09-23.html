Market Summary

Daily Overview

  • The Indian stock market on 23 September 2025 witnessed mixed and volatile performance with the BSE Sensex closing slightly lower by 57.87 points (0.07%) at 82,102.10 after touching an intra-day low around 81,776, reflecting cautious investor sentiment.
  • Nifty50 traded below 25,200 by market close, recovering from earlier losses but overall showing signs of dented retail investor confidence amid macroeconomic concerns.
  • Profit booking was visible across indices with IT sector notably down by about 3%, while energy sector showed relative strength gaining approximately 7%, reflecting divergent investor preferences.
  • Mid and small-cap stocks exhibited comparatively more weakness, indicating selective risk aversion among retail investors and fund managers on this day.
  • Market movements were influenced broadly by global uncertainties and domestic policy developments, including concerns about rising US H-1B visa costs and their impact on Indian IT outsourcing firms.

Sector Highlights

PSU Bank +1.09%

PSU Bank sector rose 1.09% led by a rebound in Bank Nifty, recovering about 500 points from day's low, driven by positive investor sentiment on government initiatives supporting public sector lending and expectations of improved asset quality amidst stable macroeconomic outlook.

Metal +1.00%

Metal sector gained 1.00% supported by a rise in global commodity prices including steel and aluminium, and upbeat domestic demand outlook. Metals stocks rose following positive order wins in related infrastructure projects and a firming in raw material prices.

Auto +0.62%

Auto sector increased by 0.62%, bolstered by improved quarterly earnings from major auto manufacturers and expectations of sustained demand recovery. Additionally, easing supply chain disruptions and favorable export orders contributed to modest gains.

Realty -0.89%

Realty sector declined 0.89% on profit booking after recent rallies and concerns over rising construction costs due to increasing prices of key inputs like cement and steel. Slower new project launches and cautious buyer sentiment amid financing cost hike also weighed.

FMCG -1.29%

FMCG sector dropped 1.29% impacted by margin pressures from rising commodity costs such as edible oils and packaging materials, coupled with moderation in rural demand growth. Profit booking in select large-cap FMCG stocks after recent outperformance contributed to the decline.
