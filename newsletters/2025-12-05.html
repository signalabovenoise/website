Sector Highlights

PSU Bank +1.51%

PSU Banks gained 1.51% driven by the Reserve Bank of India's announcement of a 25 basis points rate cut, improving lending margins and credit growth prospects for public sector banks. Additionally, sector-specific buying was supported by easing concerns on asset quality and government assurances of capital infusion.

IT +0.90%

The IT sector rose 0.90% following positive global cues on technology spending and renewed optimism around India-US trade negotiations, which could ease export tariffs impacting Indian IT exports. Some IT companies also reported steady earnings outlooks, supporting the sector's modest gains.

Bank +0.82%

The broader banking sector rose 0.82%, reflecting RBI's rate cut boosting loan demand and reducing funding costs. Strong buying interest in retail and corporate lenders accompanied positive corporate earnings updates and stable asset quality trends.

MNC -0.21%

MNC stocks declined 0.21%, pressured by the continued depreciation of the Indian rupee against the US dollar, which affects profitability of companies with high foreign currency exposure. Profit booking was seen after recent sharp gains amid cautious investor sentiment.

Media -0.48%

The Media sector fell 0.48%, impacted by weak advertising revenue forecasts amid slowing consumer spending. Regulatory scrutiny over digital content and licensing issues also contributed to the negative sentiment.

Top News Developments
RBI's Surprise Rate Cut Ignites Market Rally
  • The RBI unexpectedly cut the repo rate by 25 basis points, citing cooling inflation and improved growth prospects, which immediately boosted market sentiment.
  • This was supplemented by significant liquidity measures, including Open Market Operation (OMO) purchases of ₹1 lakh crore, to ensure ample credit availability in the economy.
  • The rate cut directly fueled a rally in rate-sensitive sectors like banking, auto, and real estate, leading the Nifty to close above 26,100.
  • However, the move also caused the Indian rupee to weaken past 90 against the US dollar, reflecting the trade-off between stimulating growth and currency stability.
  • Despite the headline index gains, market breadth was mixed with small-cap indices declining, suggesting that investors are being selective and cautious.
BAT to Offload ITC Hotels Stake in Major Block Deal
  • British American Tobacco (BAT) is selling a stake of up to 7% in ITC Hotels through a block deal valued at nearly ₹2,998 crore.
  • This move is likely part of BAT's broader strategy to rebalance its global portfolio and raise capital by divesting what it may consider a non-core asset.
  • The introduction of such a large quantum of shares into the market could lead to increased supply and short-term price volatility for ITC Hotels' stock.
  • For retail investors, the deal's success will be a barometer of institutional interest in the hospitality sector's ongoing recovery.
  • This event could also trigger further consolidation or strategic realignments within the Indian hospitality and real estate investment landscape.
Tata Power's Mundra Plant Poised for Operational Restart
  • Tata Power's announcement that its major Mundra thermal plant will soon resume operations is a significant positive catalyst for the company and the power sector.
  • The restart implies that critical operational hurdles, such as fuel supply or regulatory clearances, have been successfully resolved, boosting India's energy security.
  • For Tata Power, this development directly translates to improved revenue generation, stronger cash flows, and enhanced earnings visibility going forward.
  • A stable supply from a large-scale plant benefits the broader economy, particularly energy-intensive sectors like manufacturing and chemicals which depend on reliable power.
  • This news solidifies a bullish medium-term outlook for Tata Power and reinforces stability across the power generation and distribution value chain.
SEBI Cracks Down on Unregistered Financial Influencers
  • SEBI took decisive action by banning financial influencer Avadhut Sathe and his academy for providing unregistered investment advisory services.
  • The regulator also ordered a massive disgorgement of ₹546 crore, sending a powerful message against unauthorized financial advice.
  • This crackdown highlights the growing regulatory scrutiny on 'finfluencers' who operate outside the legal framework and often prey on unsuspecting investors.
  • The action serves as a crucial reminder for retail investors to always verify the SEBI registration of any individual or entity offering investment advice.
  • This move is expected to improve the integrity of the market by weeding out bad actors and protecting investors from fraudulent schemes.
