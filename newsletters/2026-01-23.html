Market Summary

Daily Overview

  • Indian equity markets experienced heightened volatility throughout the week leading up to January 23, 2026, with the Nifty 50 logging its steepest weekly drop in four months driven by tepid earnings and sustained foreign institutional investor outflows.
  • The Nifty 50 declined approximately 1,400 points over 12 sessions and entered extreme oversold territory on the daily chart, with key support identified at 25,000–24,800 and immediate resistance at 25,400–25,500.
  • Negative market sentiment was primarily influenced by weak global cues, concerns surrounding US trade tariff policies, geopolitical issues related to Greenland, and significant pressure on the Indian rupee, which hit a fresh record low of USD/INR 91.74.
  • Despite Thursday's recovery where the Sensex and Nifty snapped a three-day losing streak and rose nearly 1% aided by global risk-on sentiment following US tariff rollbacks against European allies, Friday morning saw the Sensex fall over 250 points amid volatile trading.
  • Retail investor sentiment remained cautious with elevated volatility and limited risk appetite, though technical analysis suggests a pullback may be likely in the near term with consolidation expected within the 25,500–24,800 range.

No IT-specific news drivers were identified in available reports; the decline reflects participation in the overall market downturn driven by sustained FII selling and weakness in banking and Adani Group stocks.

FMCG -0.61%

Moderate decline of -0.61% amid sector-wide selloff affecting all Indian sectors. The decline reflects general market weakness rather than FMCG-specific headwinds. However, positive underlying demand signals emerged with India's Flash PMI rising to 59.5 in January from 57.8 in December, indicating improved private sector growth, which could provide support for consumption-linked FMCG companies medium-term.

Metal -0.83%

Decline of -0.83% as the sector participated in broad market selling. Metal sector weakness reflects the overall market downturn, though commodity prices showed mixed signals with gold, silver, and platinum hitting fresh record highs driven by geopolitical uncertainties and weaker dollar expectations, which could benefit precious metals but weighed on base metals amid broad equity market selloff.

Media -2.51%

Sharp decline of -2.51%, among the worst-performing sectors alongside Realty. Media sector weakness was part of a broad-based selloff with no media-specific catalysts identified in available reports. The sector's outperformance to the downside likely reflects its sensitivity to advertising demand and consumer discretionary spending in a risk-off market environment dominated by FII selling.

Realty -3.34%

Steepest decline of -3.34%, leading the market downturn alongside Media. Realty sector underperformance reflects heightened sensitivity to interest rate expectations and FII capital outflows during the sustained foreign selling pattern dominating markets in early 2026. With the Union Budget scheduled for February 1st, investors are awaiting potential policy measures affecting real estate demand and financing costs.

Top News Developments
Amagi's Historic IPO Listing Signals New Era for Cloud-Native SaaS in India
  • Amagi's listing marks a significant milestone for India's digital transformation, validating specialized SaaS business models beyond traditional IT services and potentially attracting further capital into cloud infrastructure and media-tech segments.
  • The IPO's success, particularly before the Union Budget, signals strong investor confidence in technology-driven enterprises, potentially unlocking a new wave of cloud-native SaaS companies seeking public markets and expanding India's digital economy.
  • This development positions Amagi as a key beneficiary of India's accelerated digital infrastructure adoption and policy support for technology, suggesting a bullish narrative for related sectors like enterprise software, data center operators, and IT infrastructure.
  • For retail investors, this trend highlights the growing opportunities in niche technology sectors that leverage cloud solutions, urging a focus on companies with strong recurring revenue models and scalability.
  • The successful listing could also influence valuations for other private SaaS companies in India, setting a precedent for future technology IPOs and shaping investor expectations for growth-oriented tech stocks.
Premier Energies Boosts India's Solar Manufacturing with 400 MW Capacity Expansion
  • Premier Energies' new facility significantly strengthens India's domestic renewable energy manufacturing, aligning perfectly with the Production-Linked Incentive (PLI) scheme and reducing reliance on imports, which bolsters national energy security.
  • This expansion supports India's ambitious 2030 clean energy targets, indicating robust growth prospects for the entire renewable energy ecosystem, including equipment suppliers, EPC contractors, and project developers.
  • Increased domestic solar cell production is likely to influence solar equipment pricing dynamics, potentially making solar projects more cost-effective and accelerating adoption across utility-scale and rooftop segments for commercial and residential users.
  • The development signals continued government focus on capital expenditure in renewables, a theme expected to be reinforced in the upcoming Union Budget, providing multi-year policy tailwinds for companies like NTPC and Adani Green Energy.
  • Retail investors should recognize the long-term bullish outlook for the renewable energy infrastructure theme, considering companies that are direct beneficiaries of domestic manufacturing incentives and increasing clean energy mandates.
Persistent FII Selling vs. Resilient DII Buying Creates Market Crosscurrents
  • FIIs continue to be net sellers, offloading significant equity volumes due to global tariff uncertainties, geopolitical concerns, and a rotation of capital to developed markets, creating sustained liquidity headwinds for Indian large-cap stocks. This ongoing outflow contributed to the Indian Rupee hitting an all-time low against the US dollar, reflecting broader pressure on the forex market.
  • Conversely, Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) have maintained a strong buying streak, consistently channeling domestic savings into equities, which provides a crucial floor to the market but may not fully offset the FII outflows, leading to selective weakness rather than a broad-based decline.
  • This divergence creates market fragmentation, with sectors heavily favored by FIIs (like IT Services, Banking, Pharma) facing greater selling pressure, while domestically-focused sectors and quality mid/small-cap equities may show relative resilience or even outperform.
  • The market is trading in a cautious sideways range ahead of the Union Budget, with the sustainability of DII support against FII selling being a key determinant of near-term direction, pending clarity on global trade policies and interest rate trajectories.
  • Retail investors should brace for potential volatility, especially in large-cap stocks, and consider opportunities in resilient, domestically-oriented sectors, while also acknowledging that market valuations remain stretched despite recent corrections, advocating for a staggered investment approach in small and microcaps.

Policy & Regulations

  • India's Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU-IND) has issued formal notices to 25 cryptocurrency exchanges for failing to register under the Prevention of Money-Laundering Act, signaling an intensified crackdown on digital asset platforms. This regulatory move underscores the government's commitment to mitigating financial risks in the crypto market and will likely increase compliance costs for firms operating in this sector, potentially influencing broader crypto-related equity performance.
  • The upcoming Union Budget 2026 is widely anticipated to prioritize sustained capital expenditure, with a particular focus on critical sectors such as defence, railways, renewables, and manufacturing, indicating a continued push for infrastructure-led growth. While conservative tax policies are expected, market experts believe that discussions around reducing capital gains tax for equities are unlikely to significantly boost investor sentiment, as global factors and corporate earnings growth are seen as more critical market drivers.
  • Significant reforms are on the horizon for India's insurance sector, aiming to boost coverage, improve pricing mechanisms, and align with global standards, which is expected to foster sustainable growth and attract further investment into the industry. Meanwhile, SEBI has demonstrated its firm stance against market manipulation by accusing top executives from major firms like PwC and EY of insider trading in a 2022 Yes Bank share sale, emphasizing heightened scrutiny on corporate governance practices.
