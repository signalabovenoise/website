Emerging Opportunities

Utilities and power transmission after recent brokerage upgrades, because earnings visibility improved and the market still discounts regulated cash flows, creating a rerating window.
EMS exporters and electronics manufacturing, because FY26 export growth is outrunning domestic sales and global supply-chain shift supports volume expansion at scale.
Airlines and airport-linked names, because broker target hikes reflect stronger travel demand and fuel discipline, while the market still prices them as cyclical laggards.
Telecom infrastructure turns contrarian now, because network monetization and tower consolidation can improve cash generation even as investors focus on near-term subscriber pricing pressure.
Defense electronics and utilities, because institutional preference is already shifting there and policy-linked order flow can sustain earnings without depending on global risk sentiment.
