September 11, 2025

Market Summary

Daily Overview

  • On September 11, 2025, Indian equity indices ended higher with the BSE Sensex up 123.58 points (0.15%) at 81,548.73 and the NSE Nifty closing above 25,000 at 25,005.50, marking a seven-session winning streak and a three-week high for the market.
  • The market was led by strong gains in oil and gas stocks, along with positive contributions from media and PSU banking sectors, while auto and IT sectors experienced selling pressure.
  • Retail investor sentiment appeared cautiously optimistic, reflected in steady buying by domestic institutional investors (DIIs) who purchased equities worth ₹5,004.29 crore, counterbalancing foreign institutional investor (FII) net selling of ₹115.69 crore.
  • The broader market was mixed with Nifty Smallcap 100 flat and Nifty Midcap 100 up slightly by 0.08%, indicating selective engagement by investors beyond large caps.
  • The Indian rupee weakened by 34 paise to 88.44 against the US dollar, adding a cautious tone to market sentiment against a backdrop of global macroeconomic developments.

Sector Highlights

Media +1.02%

The media sector saw significant gains on September 11, 2025, driven primarily by strong buying interest in Cyber Media shares, which surged nearly 10%. This rise reflects a growing investor appetite aligned with the expanding Indian video surveillance market, valued at US$1.3 billion and growing at an annual rate of 16.5%, fueled by regulatory support and increasing demand in security applications.

PSE +0.98%

Public Sector Enterprises (PSE) stocks like NTPC and ONGC performed well, benefiting from the energy sector's buying momentum and renewed optimism in government infrastructure projects. For instance, Rail Vikas Nigam (RVNL) attracted attention on deals with West Central Railway, reflecting positive investor sentiment around government contracts and infrastructure growth.

Energy +0.88%

The energy sector exhibited strong buying interest and contributed positively to the Sensex gains, supported by crude oil prices and favorable outlooks on regulatory and demand fronts. Stocks such as NTPC, ONGC, and Waaree Energies saw rallies, with Waaree Energies up by 7%, driven by optimism over sustained demand and policy support in the oil, gas, and renewable energy segments.

Auto -0.33%

The automobile sector showed weakness on the day, with major players like Mahindra & Mahindra, Maruti Suzuki, Tata Motors, Hero MotoCorp, and Bajaj Auto witnessing profit-taking selling pressure. This trend reflects cautious investor sentiment possibly due to concerns over slowing sales momentum and tighter monetary conditions ahead despite the broader market optimism.

IT -0.50%

The IT sector delivered mixed performance. While heavyweight stocks like HCL Technologies, TCS, Tech Mahindra, and Infosys showed gains leading the Sensex upwards earlier, by the end of the day, IT stocks including Infosys and Wipro faced selling pressure. Overall, IT's earlier gains were boosted by optimism about India-US trade talks and expectations of improving earnings post-GST rationalisation and supportive monetary policy.
