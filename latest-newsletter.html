Market Summary

Daily Overview

  • The Indian stock market ended lower on 19 September 2025 as the Sensex dropped around 387 points and the Nifty50 fell below 25,350, breaking a three-day winning streak.
  • After a positive run fueled by hopes of U.S. Federal Reserve rate cuts and progress in the US-India trade negotiations, investors took profits leading to some weakness in indices.
  • Overall retail investor sentiment appeared cautious given the retreat from recent highs despite earlier optimism and substantial foreign institutional investment inflows.
  • The market capitalisation stood around Rs 1,485,192 crore, reflecting moderate contraction in overall equity value relative to the recent peak.
  • Broader market indicators hinted at sector rotation and some profit booking despite the underlying supportive macroeconomic and policy drivers.
