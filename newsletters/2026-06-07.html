Fundamentals Corner
The company’s fundamentals are weakening as a decade-long stagnant ROE of 9% and a recent 9% drop in quarterly net profits highlight a failure to convert double-digit sales growth into bottom-line value.
Growth momentum is currently decoupling, with expenses rising faster than revenue, leading to flat operating profits despite a 12.5% jump in top-line sales.
The stock’s 11% decline over the past year correctly reflects this margin erosion, as a 22 P/E is difficult to sustain for a business with deteriorating profitability and mediocre capital efficiency.

Top News Developments
The Rise of the Structural Stabilizer: 4.3 Crore New Retail Investors
  • The NSE reported the addition of 4.3 crore new investor accounts in the past year, now representing 17% of the total base.
  • This explosive growth in retail participation provides a critical buffer against persistent FII outflows and global 'risk-off' sentiment.
  • Domestic liquidity is increasingly decoupling Indian market floors from traditional foreign institutional cycles.
AI Supply Chain Pivot and IT Sector Underperformance
  • Indian IT stocks are facing pressure as investors demand evidence that global AI investments translate into revenue for service providers.
  • Capital is shifting 'lower down' the supply chain toward electronics and hardware, a trend seen in South Korea now mirroring in India.
  • A transition toward 'bottom-up' stock picking is replacing broad-based sector bets in the technology and industrial space.
Strategic Consolidation and Institutional Confidence in Infrastructure
  • Significant stake purchases in Adani companies by SBI Mutual Fund signal a consolidation of domestic institutional confidence in the conglomerate's assets.
  • Private equity interest is shifting toward transport, digital infra, and green energy, marking these as the high-conviction themes of the year.
  • Consolidation in niche segments, such as ixigo’s majority stake in Brevistay, highlights a trend of incumbents buying growth in the travel-tech sector.
