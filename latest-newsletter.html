September 24, 2025

Market Summary

Daily Overview

  • The Indian stock market on 24 September 2025 showed mild weakness with the BSE Sensex closing at around 81,700 points, down approximately 0.47%, and the Nifty 50 hovering near 25,050, down about 0.45%, marking the fourth consecutive day of declines.
  • Despite the overall slide, the Bank Nifty index was resilient, gaining around 0.41%, supported by strong buying in PSU banks, indicating selective strength within the financial sector.
  • Investor sentiment remained cautious due to persistent foreign institutional investor (FII) selling pressure and concerns related to international developments, including policy changes abroad.
  • The broader market showed weakness as midcap and smallcap indices slipped close to 1%, suggesting risk-off behavior among retail and institutional investors.
  • The market was influenced by mixed economic signals domestically, including steady but cautious central bank policies and evolving global macroeconomic uncertainties, creating a subdued trading environment.

Top News Developments
Sustained Foreign Institutional Investor Selling Amidst US H-1B Visa Fee Hike Concerns
  • FIIs sold Rs 3,551 crore worth of equities on September 23, extending selling trends exacerbated by US visa fee hikes.
  • The H-1B fee increase raises operational costs for IT firms, reducing investor confidence in export-dependent sectors.
  • Selling pressure from FIIs constrains liquidity, impacting valuations of financial and IT stocks predominantly.
  • Sectoral revaluations may redirect investor interest to domestic-focused industries as export-headwinds increase.
  • Persistent foreign outflows could eventually call for supportive measures by central regulators.
Sectoral Weakness in Realty, Auto, and Private Banks Dragging Broader Markets Lower
  • The Nifty Realty index fell 2.5% due to higher borrowing costs or regulatory pressures on project approvals.
  • Nifty Auto dropped as input cost inflation and semiconductor shortages continue to pressure manufacturers.
  • Private banks saw declines amidst concerns over tightening liquidity conditions and asset quality fears.
  • Underperformance in these sectors signals cautious investor risk appetite amidst macro uncertainties.
  • Fund rotation into defensives like FMCG stocks is evident as broader markets face sustained volatility.
Tata Investment Corporation's Strong Rally Despite Broad Market Weakness
  • Shares surged over 4%, defying broader benchmarks, driven by investor interest in well-governed holding companies.
  • The company’s diversified portfolio offers relative safety in uncertain market conditions.
  • Speculations around forthcoming corporate actions have intensified investor interest in Tata Investment Corporation.
  • A recent stock split approval highlights efforts to attract retail investors and boost liquidity.
  • This move signals selective confidence in Tata Group companies, reinforced by their governance reputation.
